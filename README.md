# OneOfMany — The Abundance & Transition Atlas

**Live Interactive Website:** [https://d-10-g.github.io/OneOfMany/](https://d-10-g.github.io/OneOfMany/)

An interactive library of **85 subtopics (170 Markdown documents: 85 Deep Reports + 85 Flexible Storyboards)** exploring how humanity can transition rapidly and compassionately to an abundant future across four foundational pillars:

1. **Abundant Energy** (`vision-01-abundant-energy` & `vision-shorter-02-abundant-energy`)
2. **Automation, Robotics & Automated Science** (`vision-01-automation` & `vision-shorter-02-automation`)
3. **Social, Economic & Institutional Transition** (`vision-01-social` & `vision-shorter-02-social`)
4. **Space & Orbital Industrialization** (`vision-01-space` & `vision-shorter-02-space`)

---

## Two Complementary Horizons

* **Vision 01 — The Grounded Bridge (`2026–2040`, 38 Pathways / 76 Documents):** Traces the verifiable physics, 2024–2026 commercial hardware milestones, Wright's Law cost curves, and institutional reforms that build a post-scarcity civilization by the 2040s.
* **Vision 02 — The Leapfrog Sprint (`2026–2030`, 47 Pathways / 94 Documents):** Probes how the late-2026 recursive intelligence explosion, self-driving microfluidic/solid-state labs, quantum-AI hybrid simulations, and bold institutional creative destruction can compress timelines into a **1-to-4-year "skip-the-landline" leapfrog**—including **9 urgent social triage pathways** addressing already-displaced workers, sociological *anomie*, street homelessness, addiction/mental health, household debt, healthcare billing, regulatory permitting, community isolation, and epistemic trust.

## Running or Rebuilding Locally

Open `index.html` directly in any modern browser, or serve locally:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8080
```

If you edit any `REPORT.md` or `STORYBOARD.md` file, rebuild the bundled search/reader catalog via:

```bash
python3 scripts/build_catalog.py
```
