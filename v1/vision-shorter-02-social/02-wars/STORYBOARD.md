## 1. THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE ANGLES (DIRECTORS' OPTIONS)

### Angle A: "The \$45 Million Salvo vs. \$180 of Electricity" (The Asymmetric Cost Flip)
* **Hook:** Open at 2:14 AM in November 2029 along a tense border. Red radar tracks show 800 incoming autonomous drones and 40 cruise missiles (**Attacker Cost: \$45,000,000**). * **Best For:** Defense analysts, policymakers, taxpayers, and general audiences worried about drone warfare and missile depletion.

### Angle B: "Nowhere to Hide on Earth" (The 5-Minute SAR Satellite Transparency Hook)
* **Hook:** Start with a military commander trying to stage mobile missile launchers under dense night cloud cover and forest canopy—only to watch an open-source **16cm Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) + AI Hyperspectral Dashboard** light up his exact coordinates globally within 4 minutes, triggering automatic supply-chain and treaty circuit breakers before a single engine starts.
* **Best For:** Geopolitics, intelligence, OSINT, and technology audiences fascinated by how planetary transparency kills the blitzkrieg.

### Angle C: "The 40-Foot Container That Broke the Siege" (Resource Independence Hook)
* **Hook:** Open on a coastal port or arid agricultural valley that used to be one naval blockade away from famine and blackouts. Follow a crane unloading three 40-foot shipping containers—a **Solar/Sodium Microgrid**, a **Reverse-Osmosis Water Unit**, and a **Containerized Haber-Bosch Green Ammonia Rig**—that synthesize clean water, crop fertilizer, and fuel right out of sunlight, air, and seawater in 48 hours.
