## 1. THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE ANGLES (DIRECTORS' OPTIONS)

### Angle A: "The \$3,530-a-Month Kitchen Table Raise" (Intimate Family Balance-Sheet Rescue)
* **Hook:** Open on Sarah (a pediatric nurse) and Kevin (an AI-displaced marketing analyst) in late 2026 staring at four brutal monthly bills totaling **\$4,850/month** before groceries: a 7.1% mortgage (\$3,150), 24.9% credit cards (\$800), student loans for a degree AI automated (\$520), and their son's appendectomy bill (\$380). Fast-forward to May 2027 as a single click on the **Sovereign Balance-Sheet Reset Portal** drops their total monthly debt nut to **\$1,320 overnight**.
* **Best For:** Broad middle- and working-class families, viral social sharing, and consumer advocacy audiences.

### Angle B: "Avoiding 1933: Why Deflating Wages + Frozen Debts Requires a Reset" (Macroeconomic Clarity)
* **Hook:** Open on Irving Fisher's famous 1933 *Debt-Deflation* chart juxtaposed with the 2026 AI productivity surge. Explain the central macroeconomic truth: when AI makes goods and labor cheaper while \$18.5 trillion in household debt stays fixed in stone, everyone defaults—unless the Central Bank swaps fixed debt into **1% Stability Notes and Shared Equity** just like FDR's Home Owners' Loan Corporation did in 1933.
* **Best For:** Economists, central bankers, investors, policymakers, and financial-press audiences.

### Angle C: "One Penny on the Dollar" (The 90-Day Abolition of Medical & Usury Traps)
* **Hook:** Hold up a single copper penny next to a \$1.00 hospital collection notice. Show how debt collectors buy medical misery for 1.5 cents on the dollar—and how in early 2027, the federal government bought and **incinerated all \$220 billion of American medical debt for \$3.5 billion in 90 days**.
* **Best For:** High-impact short-form documentary, healthcare reformers, and bipartisan populist appeal.

## 4. CINEMATOGRAPHY, SOUND DESIGN & MOTION-GRAPHIC PRODUCTION BIBLE

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **2026 Debt Stress Opening:** Tight, claustrophobic framing under harsh overhead kitchen pendant light; red overdue ink and cold calculator LCDs.
* **2027–2030 Post-Reset Reality:** Expansive, sunlit domestic warmth (35mm lenses), natural oak and linen textures, and calm emerald-and-gold sovereign ledger typography.
* **Act III (Main Street Jubilee):** Vibrant twilight street scenes showing packed neighborhood restaurants, debt-free young couples moving into modular homes, and thriving local credit unions.

* **Opening:** Suffocating, rapid mechanical adding-machine clatter and low heartbeat bass.
* **The One-Click Reset (Scene 2 & 5):** The adding-machine clatter stops dead on a single crisp finger tap—replaced by a deep, liberating orchestral exhale (warm strings, French horn, and acoustic guitar).
