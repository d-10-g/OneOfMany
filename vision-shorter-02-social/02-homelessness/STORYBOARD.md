# VISION 02 (2026–2030): ENDING STREET HOMELESSNESS IN 18–36 MONTHS
## Flexible Multi-Angle Storyboard & Creative Production Guide (`02-homelessness`)

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard translates the 18-to-36-month street-homelessness eradication blueprint of `REPORT.md` into a vivid, pragmatic visual narrative (target runtime: 3:30–5:00). It shatters the false choice between cruel criminalization and cruel sidewalk abandonment—showing how State Zoning Preemption, \$18,000 30-day Autonomous Dignity Villages, and Compassionate Tri-Track Triage end unsheltered homelessness at 42% lower cost than the 2026 status quo.

## 1. THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE ANGLES (DIRECTORS' OPTIONS)

### Angle A: "The \$800,000 Lie vs. The \$18,000 Key" (Fiscal & Engineering Shock Value)
* **Hook:** Open on a single 2026 sidewalk tent surrounded by police cruisers and an ambulance—overlaying the real taxpayer bill (**\$75,000/year to keep one person on asphalt**) next to a 6-year, **\$850,000/unit** municipal housing sign where nothing has been built. Then cut to a flatbed truck delivering four turnkey, solar-powered **\$18,000 private micro-homes** in a single afternoon.
* **Best For:** Taxpayers, mayors, governors, urban planners, and frustrated voters across every political spectrum.

### Angle B: "Three People, Three Tracks" (Ending the One-Size-Fits-All Fallacy)
* **Hook:** Follow three real faces from the same 2026 city block—**Carla** (living in her car after an AI layoff), **Marcus** (trapped in fentanyl addiction), and **Thomas** (suffering from untreated schizophrenia)—showing why treating all three the same failed, and how **Tri-Track Triage** healed each of them by 2028.
* **Best For:** Documentary storytelling, healthcare leaders, social-work reformers, and mainstream empathy.

### Angle C: "Saturday Morning at Oak Creek Village" (The *Community First!* Model Scaled 50x)
* **Hook:** Start inside a vibrant, sunlit Saturday farmer's and artisan market in 2029—handcrafted cedar furniture, organic produce, wood-fired bread, and live acoustic music—before revealing that this thriving neighborhood is an **Autonomous Dignity Village** built in 30 days for formerly unhoused citizens.
* **Best For:** Inspirational community vision, neighborhood NIMBY persuasion, and philanthropic/civic mobilization.

## 4. CINEMATOGRAPHY, SOUND DESIGN & MOTION-GRAPHIC PRODUCTION BIBLE

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **2026 Sidewalk Flashbacks:** Harsh, desaturated urban concrete grays and flashing red-and-blue emergency strobes; uncomfortable street-level wide lenses (24mm).
* **2028–2029 Dignity Village Reality:** Warm, grounded architectural photography (35mm and 50mm)—honeyed cedar wood, deep solar-panel blues, garden greens, and inviting amber porch lights at dusk.
* **Act III (The Restored City):** Crisp morning light across spotless downtown transit plazas, parks, and bustling Saturday Village markets.

### Sound Design & Musical Score Arc
* **Opening Contrast:** Replace the chaotic 2026 soundscape of sirens, traffic honks, and shouting with the tranquil morning acoustics of Oak Creek Village—birds, acoustic guitar, coffee pouring, and woodshop saws humming in the distance.
* **The Key Turn (Scene 3):** A deeply satisfying, solid brass deadbolt *click* that anchors the musical transition from tense strings to a warm, melodic Americana/chamber score.
* **Diegetic Sound Anchors:** Rescue dogs trotting on wooden porches, espresso machines at the Saturday market, and quiet garden fountains inside the Track C Restorative Sanctuary.

### Data Callout & Lower-Third Typography Rules
* Contrast **2026 Municipal Waste** directly against **30-Day Village Speed & Cost**:
  * Always pair **Unit Capital Cost & Speed** (`\$800k / 6 Years` $\rightarrow$ `\$18k / 30 Days`) with **Annual Taxpayer Operating Cost** (`\$75,000/yr on Asphalt` $\rightarrow$ `\$18,500/yr in Dignity Village`).
  * Highlight verified real-world precedents in clean lower-third citation badges:
    * `SOURCE: Community First! Village, Austin TX (86% Long-Term Housing Retention)`
    * `SOURCE: Houston Coalition for the Homeless (-63% Unsheltered via Unified Command)`
    * `SOURCE: Denver Supportive Housing RCT (-40% Jail Days, -65% Detox Visits)`
