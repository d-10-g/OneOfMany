# FLEXIBLE STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE
## Vision 01 / Pathway 1A: Commercial Fusion — *From $B^4$ Superconducting Magnets to a \$0.003/kWh World*

> **Creative Purpose:** Rather than locking production into a single rigid script, this storyboard provides **three distinct narrative entry points**, a **14-scene modular 3-act visual table**, and a **Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Bank** that filmmakers, animators, and interactive creators can remix freely.

---

## PART I: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE ANGLES (DIRECTORS' OPTIONS)

### Option A: The Human-First "Tuesday Morning in 2040" Opening
* **Hook:** Open inside a warm, recognizable coastal valley in 2040—terraced citrus groves irrigated by a crystal-clear desalination canal, a sunlit timber-and-glass vertical farm market, and a family kitchen where the monthly utility bill is a flat `\$12` connection fee—before revealing the retired coal station down the river quietly repowered by two compact fusion cores.
* **Best For:** General audiences, civic optimism campaigns, and mainstream documentaries.

### Option B: The Skeptic-Breaker "Why 'Always 30 Years Away' Just Died" Opening
* **Hook:** Name the classic cynical joke in the first five seconds (*"Fusion is 30 years away, and always will be"*), then visually contrast ITER's 10-story $840\text{ m}^3$ cavern against a $20\text{ m}^3$ 20-Tesla REBCO compact core ($P \propto B^4$), 10 kHz Deep RL plasma control, and the \$14.24B of steel already bolted to the floor in 2026.
* **Best For:** Engineers, policymakers, investors, and analytical viewers.

### Option C: The Smooth Transition "Old Bones, New Heart" Opening
* **Hook:** Follow a third-generation utility worker at a retiring coal plant in Tennessee, Virginia, the UK, or Bavaria as the smokestacks come down, the existing gigawatt switchyard and steam turbines stay in place, and a factory-built fusion heat module arrives by flatbed transport—preserving hometown jobs while eliminating 8 years of grid queues.
* **Best For:** Energy transition stories, labor/community town halls, and regional economic features.

## PART III: VISUAL METAPHOR & B-ROLL MOTIF BANK

1. **The Cathedral vs. The Jet Engine:** Side-by-side volumetric wireframe comparing ITER's $840\text{ m}^3$ vacuum vessel to a $20\text{ m}^3$ REBCO compact core and a commercial aircraft turbofan.
2. **The Postage-Stamp Fuel Safety Demo:** A physicist holding a tiny glass vial representing the `<1 gram` of plasma inside a fusion chamber at any instant—visually proving why a runaway meltdown is physically impossible.
3. **The Gallon Pitcher vs. 7 Oil Barrels:** A tabletop visual placing one glass gallon jug of Pacific seawater next to seven 42-gallon barrels of crude oil (~300 gallons of gasoline equivalent).
4. **The Switchyard Plug-In:** Time-lapse graphic of a retiring coal plant where the coal pile and smokestack fade out, a compact fusion hall slides in, and the existing high-voltage transmission lines stay lit without interruption.
