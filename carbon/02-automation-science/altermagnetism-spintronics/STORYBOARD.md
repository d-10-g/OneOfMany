# Storyboard & Production Guide: Altermagnetism & Terahertz Spintronics
**Pathway:** [`altermagnetism-spintronics`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Make a brand-new chapter of high-school physics textbooks feel immediate, tactile, and revolutionary. Show how **Altermagnetism**—winner of the **2026 Europhysics Prize** and proved in **SLAC's chiral $\text{MnTe}$** X-ray movies—breaks a 100-year magnetic catch-22 to deliver **1,000× faster terahertz memory**, **100× tighter zero-stray-field packing**, and electronics freed from conflict cobalt and rare heavy metals.

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "Tearing Up Chapter 12 of Physics" (Wonder & Paradigm-Shift Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** A worn physics textbook opens to *"The Two Types of Magnetism: Ferromagnets & Antiferromagnets."* Suddenly a luminous third crystal column rises straight out of the page—zero external iron filings cling to its outside (zero stray field!), yet inside, crimson spin-up and sapphire spin-down electron highways race cleanly at **terahertz speed**.
* **Target Viewer:** General science viewers, students, educators, and hardware enthusiasts.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Basic condensed-matter physics is finished; chip scaling is out of fundamental tricks."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"How did physicists overlook a third fundamental branch of magnetism hiding in plain sight inside everyday crystals for a hundred years?"*

### Opening Option B: "Why Fridge Magnets Make Terrible Neighbors" (Direct Cynic-Buster Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Playful tabletop demo: pack tiny bar magnets too close together and they violently snap, flip, and scramble each other's orientation (why ferromagnetic MRAM hits a density wall). Swap in **altermagnetic $\text{MnTe}$ tiles**—they sit shoulder-to-shoulder at nanometer spacing in calm silence while switching $1,000\times$ faster.
* **Target Viewer:** Chip architects, data-center engineers, and tech skeptics.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Magnetic non-volatile memory will never match DRAM density or speed."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What happens when memory bits become completely invisible to their neighbors' magnetic fields—yet crystal-clear to read electrically?"*

### Opening Option C: "Memory Without Rare Earths or Standby Waste" (Sustainability Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Contrast a smoky diesel backup generator cooling leaky server DRAM racks with Kwame's silent, instant-on solar diagnostic server in Accra—built from earth-abundant manganese and chromium crystals with **0.00 watts** of idle memory drain.
* **Target Viewer:** Climate advocates, Global South technologists, and supply-chain policymakers.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Next-generation chips always demand rarer, dirtier, more monopolized minerals."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"How did crystal symmetry let abundant everyday minerals outperform scarce platinum and conflict cobalt?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Geometric symmetry choreography—showing rotated atomic octahedra splitting crimson and sapphire spin rivers inside crystal lattices, paired with SLAC synchrotron X-ray beams.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: "The Rotated Twin Dance (Spin-Group Symmetry)"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Two dancers spinning in opposite directions ($\uparrow$ and $\downarrow$) stand inside elliptical spotlights rotated $90^\circ$ to each other—from far overhead their net spin cancels to zero, yet north-south vs. east-west traffic flows in pure single-spin lanes.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Demystifies $d$-wave and $g$-wave momentum-dependent spin splitting without equations.
* **Data Ticker:** `Macroscopic Stray Field: 0.00 Tesla` | `Momentum-Resolved Spin Polarization: High (~1 eV)`.

### Metaphor 2: "Subway Turnstiles: Nanoseconds vs. Picoseconds"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Heavy cast-iron vault doors groaning open once per second (gigahertz ferromagnetic domain walls) vs. frictionless optical turnstiles flicking **1,000 times** in that same second (terahertz chiral altermagnons).
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Makes the $1,000\times$ speed leap from $\text{GHz}$ to $\text{THz}$ visceral.
* **Data Ticker:** `Ferromagnetic MRAM: ~1 Nanosecond` $\rightarrow$ `Altermagnetic Switching: ~1 Picosecond (1,000x Faster)`.

### Metaphor 3: "The Leaky Bucket (DRAM) vs. The Sealed Crystal (Alt-MRAM)"
* **Visual Mechanics:** A server worker furiously bailing water back into a leaky sieve 15,000 times a second (DRAM capacitor refresh) vs. a crystal prism holding light indefinitely with zero effort.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Shows why non-volatile universal memory slashes global data-center and mobile standby power.
* **Data Ticker:** `DRAM Refresh Tax: Continuous Watts` vs. `Altermagnetic Standby Power: 0 Watts`.

### Metaphor 4: "Abundant Crust vs. Rare Dust"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Two heaps of Earth's crust: a tiny pinhead of platinum/iridium next to an abundant mountain of manganese, iron, and chromium.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Connects spin-group physics directly to ethical, geopolitically resilient supply chains.
* **Data Ticker:** `Platinum Crustal Abundance: ~0.005 ppm` vs. `Manganese / Iron / Chromium: 100–50,000+ ppm`.
