# Storyboard & Production Guide: Neighborhood Fab-Commons
**Pathway:** [`local-microfactories`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Tap into every viewer's righteous frustration with flimsy, unrepairable appliances, \$140 discontinued plastic latches, sweatshop fast-fashion mountains, and dead strip malls. Show a boarded-up big-box store reborn as a warm, bustling **Municipal Fab-Commons & Bakery-Café**—where **Divergent 3D** metal nodes, **Prusa/Bambu** print farms, **Shima Seiki** 3D knitters, and **Daisy/Molg** unscrewing robots turn yesterday's broken cans and toasters into custom e-bikes, \$4.50 spare parts, and \$65 kids' prosthetics two blocks from home.

---

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "The \$2 Broken Latch vs. The \$700 Landfill Washer" (Universal Frustration Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** A frustrated homeowner holds up a tiny cracked \$2 plastic door latch—while two movers wheel an otherwise spotless 180-pound washing machine toward a landfill dumpster because the OEM marked the part *"Discontinued."* Rewind the tape! Scan the latch's QR **Digital Product Passport** at a sunny corner **Fab-Commons**, print a stronger carbon-PEEK replacement in 12 minutes for **\$4.50**, and snap the washer back to life for another decade.
* **Target Viewer:** Every homeowner, renter, Right-to-Repair fan, and consumer sick of planned obsolescence.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Modern manufacturing will always force disposable junk onto consumers."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"Why do we throw away seven-hundred-dollar machines over two-dollar plastic gears—and what happens when every neighborhood can print any spare part in fifteen minutes?"*

### Opening Option B: "From Boarded-Up Big Box to Saturday Morning Commons" (Civic Revival Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Drone push-in on an ugly, weed-choked vacant suburban electronics superstore in 2025—dissolving into Saturday morning 2031 in Malmö: families sipping cardamom buns in front of silent glass bays where robots knit custom zero-waste sweaters in 28 minutes, sinter recycled soda-can e-bike lugs, and fit an 11-year-old's custom \$65 bionic arm before lunch.
* **Target Viewer:** Small-town mayors, main-street advocates, librarians, and families.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Local manufacturing died forty years ago and can never compete on price."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What happens to dead strip malls when software-defined micro-cells need zero million-dollar steel dies?"*

### Opening Option C: "8,000 Miles of Bunker Fuel vs. Speed-of-Light Bits" (Climate & Supply-Chain Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Follow a pair of shoes across 9,000 miles of smokestack container ships, port cranes, and desert fast-fashion burn piles (where 30% of sewn clothes go unsold). Smash-cut to a Milan designer emailing a 4-megabyte file at the speed of light to a local **3D WholeGarment knitter** two blocks from the buyer's apartment—zero scraps, zero ships.
* **Target Viewer:** Climate advocates, fashion reformers, and industrial designers.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Globalized sweatshops are the only way to make affordable clothes and furniture."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"As MIT's Neil Gershenfeld asks: why ship atoms across oceans when cities can transmit bits at light speed and build from local circular materials?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Warm Scandinavian woodshop + espresso-bar community energy paired with mesmerizing robotic precision behind clean glass bays.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: The "8,500-Mile Red Noodle vs. 2-Block Green Dot" Globe
* **Visual Mechanics:** Trace a conventional toaster gear's chaotic 8,500-mile red shipping line (oil well $\rightarrow$ resin plant $\rightarrow$ overseas mold $\rightarrow$ bunker ship $\rightarrow$ port truck $\rightarrow$ warehouse); then tap it and watch it collapse into a tiny 2-block green glow between your home and the corner Fab-Commons, connected only by a beam of light.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Visualizes *"ship bits, print atoms locally"* in three seconds.
* **Data Ticker:** `7,500 Miles of Bunker Fuel` $\rightarrow$ `0.4 Miles + 14 Kilobytes`.

### Metaphor 2: The "QR Passport Unlocking the Locked Vault"
* **Visual Mechanics:** A broken blender base displays a glowing **EU Digital Product Passport QR code**; scanning it pops open a corporate vault door, releasing a golden 3D gear wireframe straight into a neighborhood printer.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Shows how smart policy + microfactories kill planned obsolescence together.
* **Data Ticker:** `"Part Discontinued" Rendered Extinct`.

### Metaphor 3: The "Toaster-to-E-Bike Infinite Figure-Eight"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Continuous loop animation where a retired blender and crushed soda cans enter a **Molg/Daisy** disassembly bay on the left side of the infinity loop and emerge on the right side as sintered aluminum e-bike nodes and nylon pedals.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Makes urban mining feel immediate, clean, and neighborhood-scaled.
* **Data Ticker:** `>90% Local Material Circularity` | `Zero Landfill Shredder Fluff`.

### Metaphor 4: The "Library Card for Atoms" Wallet Shot
* **Visual Mechanics:** Close-up of a citizen's ordinary public library card tapping a terminal—illuminating \$500,000 of shared laser metal printers, CNC timber routers, and 3D looms behind glass.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Reframes advanced robotics as a democratic public utility like Carnegie libraries.
* **Data Ticker:** `Access Barrier: 1 Public Library Card + Raw Material Weight`.

## Director's Execution, Sound & Pacing Notes

* **Acoustic Palette:** Warm woodworking shavings, gentle rhythmic 3D knitting clicks, espresso steam wands, and cheerful community workshop chatter.
* **Color & Lighting:** Sunlit birch plywood, brushed recycled aluminum silver, vibrant cobalt custom prosthetics, and warm bakery amber.
* **Key Interview Archetypes:**
  1. *Municipal Fab-Lab / Repair-Café Coordinator* in Malmö, Barcelona, or Vienna holding rescued decades-old appliances.
  2. *Pediatric Prosthetist* showing how a \$65 local 90-minute socket changes life for growing children.
  3. *Generative Hardware Engineer* holding a featherweight bone-like sintered aluminum bike/car node.
* **Modular Edit Cuts:**
  * *60-Second Social Reel:* Opening A (\$2 Latch vs. \$700 Washer) $\rightarrow$ Scene 3 (Samir's \$65 Bionic Arm) $\rightarrow$ Scene 5 (Divergent Metal Node) $\rightarrow$ Metaphor 4 (Library Card).
  * *6-Minute Documentary Arc:* Complete 12-scene story from dead big-box malls to thriving circular neighborhood Fab-Commons.
* **Interactive Atlas Widget Tie-In:** Slider showing `Town Landfill E-Waste Tons & Maritime Bunker Emissions` plunging as `Local Fab-Commons Share` rises.
