# Storyboard & Production Guide: Helpful Mechanical Hands
**Pathway:** [`general-purpose-humanoids`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Counter the Hollywood cliché of cold chrome terminators and viral teleoperated stunts. Show warm, acoustic-knit, force-compliant humanoid teammates grounded in real 2024–2026 Vision-Language-Action ($\pi_0$, GR00T, Gemini Robotics) breakthroughs, \$12,000–\$18,000 EV supply-chain economics, and dignified human moments in Fukuoka homes and worker-owned job sites.

---

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "The Crumpled Towel Test" (Skeptic-to-Believer Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Open on a comical 2015 DARPA Robotics Challenge montage of clunky metal robots toppling slowly in parking lots. Smash-cut to a warm, quiet 2026 lab where a researcher dumps a tangled basket of mismatched socks, bath towels, and delicate wine glasses onto a table—and a VLA-powered robot folds every towel neatly and stacks the stemware without a single line of task-specific code.
* **Target Viewer:** Jaded tech observers and engineers who remember decades of brittle robotics demos.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Robots only work in bolted-down, pre-scripted factory demos."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"For forty years, folding a crumpled shirt stumped supercomputers. What happened when robots suddenly gained common-sense physical brains?"*

### Opening Option B: "Two Cups of Hojicha in Fukuoka" (Intimate Human Dignity Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Morning light through shoji paper in Fukuoka, 2031. A 79-year-old retired shipwright rises steadily with the gentle, soft-knit arm of a 30-kilogram 1X-class home assistant spotting his balance. When his daughter arrives on her bicycle, the heavy futon is already sunning and tea is poured—she hugs her father instead of straining her spine over a bathtub.
* **Target Viewer:** Families navigating aging parents, healthcare workers, and care-economy policymakers.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Robotic care feels cold and dystopian compared to human care."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What if a \$110-a-month mechanical helping hand is the exact thing that keeps millions of grandparents in the homes they love—and lets daughters be daughters again?"*

### Opening Option C: "An EV on Two Legs" (Economic & Engineering Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Overhead exploded-view bench shot: an electric car motor, smartphone camera array, and pouch battery slide smoothly across concrete and click together into the torso and limbs of a modern humanoid—dropping the price tag ticker from \$250,000 down to \$14,000 in five seconds.
* **Target Viewer:** Tradespeople, union stewards, municipal leaders, and small-business cooperatives.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Humanoids will always cost a quarter-million dollars and belong only to mega-corporations."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"When electric-vehicle supply chains drop the cost of a tireless physical teammate below a used compact car, how do working crews own the abundance?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Eye-level 35mm naturalism in homes; macro tactile intimacy on fingertips and tendons; zero ominous low-angle sci-fi framing.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: The "EV-to-Humanoid Morph" Exploded Diagram
* **Visual Mechanics:** A compact electric hatchback dissolves in clean studio air into its commodity parts—28 brushless stators, 1 silicon-anode pouch pack, 6 automotive CMOS cameras, and 1 edge AI chip—then reassembles smoothly into a \$14,000 humanoid frame.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Explains in six seconds why humanoid costs dropped 10x faster than aerospace forecasts predicted.
* **Data Ticker:** `2022 Lab Prototype: $150,000+` $\rightarrow$ `2026 EV Supply-Chain BOM: $16,950` $\rightarrow$ `2029 Mass Target: $6,880`.

### Metaphor 2: The "Zero-Retrofit Doorway" Architectural Overlay
* **Visual Mechanics:** A glowing blueprint wireframe compares the 60-centimeter shoulder width and stride height of a human nurse, a narrow century-old spiral staircase, a standard chemistry fume hood, and a bipedal assistant.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Demonstrates why two legs and two hands unlock \$100 trillion of existing homes, clinics, and workshops without tearing down a single wall or installing factory rails.
* **Data Ticker:** `Infrastructure Retrofit Cost: $0` | `Compatible Human Tools: 100%`.

### Metaphor 3: The "Compliant Touch vs. Rigid Gear" Split-Frame
* **Visual Mechanics:** Left frame shows a legacy 2010 caged robot arm accidentally crushing an aluminum can; right frame shows a soft tendon-driven humanoid arm gently catching a falling soap bubble and yielding effortlessly when nudged by a toddler's elbow.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Viscerally cures "Terminator anxiety" by showing passive mechanical safety in action.
* **Data Ticker:** `ISO 13482 Intrinsic Force Limit` | `500 Hz Tactile Slip Response`.

### Metaphor 4: The "Weekly Family Time Clock" Radial Chart
* **Visual Mechanics:** A warm radial 168-hour family week dial where grey blocks of *laundry, bathroom scrubbing, heavy lifting, and caregiver exhaustion* (16+ hours/week) illuminate gold and flow into *unhurried dinners, garden craft, rest, and multi-generational connection*.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Reframes robotics from "corporate GDP metrics" to reclaimed Tuesday evenings and pain-free lower backs.
* **Data Ticker:** `+16 Hours / Week Reclaimed per Household` | `-75% Tradesperson Orthopedic Injuries`.

## Director's Execution, Sound & Pacing Notes

* **Acoustic Palette:** Banish Hollywood servo whines and metallic stomps; emphasize soft wool-knit fabric rustles, whisper-quiet ($<38\text{ dBA}$) brushless hums, boiling *hojicha* kettles, timber saws, and warm human laughter.
* **Color & Lighting:** Shoot residential and cooperative scenes in warm natural morning light (cedar, oatmeal acoustic textiles, sunlit workshop pine) contrasted with crisp cyan telemetry callouts in Act II lab sequences.
* **Key Interview Archetypes:**
  1. *Embodied VLA Researcher* demonstrating zero-shot laundry folding without teleoperation rigs.
  2. *Fukuoka / Odense Home-Care Nurse* explaining how robotic transfer spotting ended her chronic back pain.
  3. *Worker-Owner Master Carpenter* showing how a credit-union 3-robot crew doubled affordable timber housing output.
* **Modular Edit Cuts:**
  * *60-Second Social Cut:* Opening A $\rightarrow$ Scene 5 (VLA Brain) $\rightarrow$ Scene 8 (\$14k EV BOM) $\rightarrow$ Scene 2 (Fukuoka Tea) $\rightarrow$ Scene 12.
  * *6-Minute Documentary Cut:* Full 12-scene arc from Fukuoka dawn to global cooperative ownership.
