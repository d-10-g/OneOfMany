# Storyboard & Production Guide: Streets Returned to People
**Pathway:** [`autonomous-ground-mobility`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Overturn both the "self-driving cars will never work" myth and the "robotaxis just mean more traffic jams" fear. Anchor in hard late-2026 reality (**Waymo's 500,000+ weekly paid rides** and **Swiss Re's 85%+ injury-crash drop**), then show the breathtaking urban transformation when \$0.25/mile electric pods + rail feeders let cities turn **30% of asphalt parking craters into shaded Barcelona-style Superblocks, parks, and timber housing**.

---

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "The 95% Empty Asphalt Time-Lapse" (Urban Awakening Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Drone hover 300 feet above a typical American downtown in 2024: paint every surface parking lot, garage, and curb-parking lane bright crimson—revealing **one-third of the entire city** paved for metal boxes that sit empty 23 hours a day. Then dissolve the red asphalt forward to 2031 into green oak canopies, sidewalk cafés, mass-timber apartments, and a single calm lane of whisper-quiet autonomous pods.
* **Target Viewer:** Urbanists, housing advocates, bike/transit fans, and car-culture skeptics.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Autonomous vehicles are an anti-urban gadget that only worsens sprawl."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What does your neighborhood look like when you reclaim the thirty percent of city land currently paved for empty parked cars?"*

### Opening Option B: "The Ball From Behind the Van" (Visceral Safety Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Dusk on a rainy street. A child's soccer ball bounces out from behind a parked truck. Freeze time: switch into a 360-degree **LiDAR + 4D Doppler Radar** view that already tracked the child's footsteps underneath the truck chassis two seconds before human eyes could see anything—bringing the pod to a gentle, effortless stop.
* **Target Viewer:** Parents, safety regulators, and skeptics who remember early AV headlines.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Robots can't react to unpredictable human streets."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"Human distraction kills 1.2 million people on roads every year. What happens now that Swiss Re insurance data proves driverless cars cut injury crashes by over eighty-five percent?"*

### Opening Option C: "Keys Without Sight" (Human Independence Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Blind Austin music therapist Marcus Vance finishes teaching cello at 5:15 PM, taps his watch, steps onto a flat ramp into a warm **Zoox** lounge pod for \$2.10, and rides effortlessly to meet friends under string-lit live oaks—zero favors asked, zero \$12,000 car-loan burden.
* **Target Viewer:** Disability advocates, aging seniors, and working families crushed by car payments.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Self-driving is a toy for Silicon Valley commuters."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What is mobility independence worth to forty-five million blind, disabled, and aging citizens locked out of car-dependent suburbs?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Eye-level street joy (sidewalk diners, kids chalking Superblocks, quiet rain gardens) contrasted with crisp $360^\circ$ point-cloud safety overlays.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: The "23 Hours Asleep" Car Clock
* **Visual Mechanics:** A 24-hour clock wrapped around a \$48,000 private SUV: 23 hours sit greyed-out in expensive asphalt stalls while household cash burns; switch to a shared electric pod and 16 golden hours carry neighbors, students, and seniors at \$0.25/mile.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Reveals why private car ownership is humanity's most underutilized capital asset.
* **Data Ticker:** `Private Car Idle Time: 95.8%` vs. `Shared Pod Utilization: 68%+`.

### Metaphor 2: The "10 Parked Cars Morph Into 1 Pod + 1 Pocket Park"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Overhead street block with 10 parked cars lining both curbs; 9 cars dissolve into green street trees, protected bike lanes, and outdoor café tables while 1 circulating pod serves all 10 households on demand.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Connects shared autonomy directly to livable, human-scale urbanism.
* **Data Ticker:** `1 Shared Autonomous Pod Replaces 8–12 Parked Vehicles`.

### Metaphor 3: The "Human Cone of Vision vs. 360° LiDAR Sphere"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Top-down intersection view showing a tired human driver's narrow $60^\circ$ forward cone (momentarily glancing down at a phone) vs. an unbroken $360^\circ$, 300-meter multi-sensor safety sphere tracking 80 pedestrians, dogs, and cyclists simultaneously.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Explains visually why Swiss Re measured an 85%+ crash-injury reduction.
* **Data Ticker:** `Reaction Latency: 0.05s Machine vs. 1.2s Distracted Human`.

### Metaphor 4: The "Ambulance Zipper Corridor"
* **Visual Mechanics:** An approaching ambulance broadcasts an encrypted V2X pulse 400 meters ahead, causing every autonomous vehicle on the avenue to glide synchronously toward the curb two blocks before the siren even arrives.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Shows emergent fleet coordination impossible for uncoordinated human traffic.
* **Data Ticker:** `Emergency Trauma Response Time: -40%`.

## Director's Execution, Sound & Pacing Notes

* **Acoustic Palette:** Dramatic mid-film drop from honking horns, screeching brakes, and combustion roar (-12 dB) into acoustic guitar, fountain water, bicycle bells, and soft tire whisper.
* **Color & Lighting:** Sun-dappled live-oak greens, warm mass-timber honey tones, and clean cyan LiDAR point-cloud geometry.
* **Key Interview Archetypes:**
  1. *Blind Daily Waymo/Zoox Commuter* describing spontaneous independent errands for the first time in life.
  2. *Emergency Room Trauma Surgeon* reflecting on what an 85% drop in road crashes means on Friday nights.
  3. *Municipal Urban Designer* standing inside an infill park built over a retired downtown parking garage.
* **Modular Edit Cuts:**
  * *60-Second Social Reel:* Opening A (Red Parking Map $\rightarrow$ Green Park) $\rightarrow$ Scene 4 (500k Rides/Wk) $\rightarrow$ Scene 5 (Swiss Re -85%) $\rightarrow$ Scene 2 (Marcus).
  * *6-Minute Documentary Arc:* Complete 12-scene transformation from asphalt craters to Vision Zero green boulevards.
* **Interactive Atlas Widget Tie-In:** City slider converting `% Shared Autonomous + Rail Mode Share` into `Acres of New Parkland & Annual Household Dollars Saved`.
