## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "The Blind Tunnel Exit" (Visceral Life-Saving Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Dramatic split windshield camera exiting a pitch-dark Alpine tunnel straight into blinding wet-road sun glare: on the left, a standard 60-fps camera whites out completely for half a second—hiding a 7-year-old chasing a soccer ball; on the right, a **Prophesee/Sony Event Retina** renders the child's crisp neon-cyan motion spikes in **$50\text{ microseconds}$** ($>120\text{ dB}$) and triggers calm braking before a human eye can even twitch.
* **Target Viewer:** Drivers, parents, automotive safety engineers, and general viewers.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Autonomous sensors always get fooled by harsh sunlight, rain glare, or sudden shadows."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"Why were we trusting 19th-century movie frames to drive cars—when dragonfly-inspired pixels can see straight into sun and shadow simultaneously?"*

### Opening Option B: "The Dragonfly vs. The Space Heater" (Direct Cynic-Buster Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Macro slow-motion of a dragonfly snatching a mosquito mid-air in 3 milliseconds using a brain the size of a sesame seed burning **0.01 milliwatts**—cut against a hot, buzzing edge GPU draining a drone battery in eight minutes just re-reading an empty static wall 60 times a second.
* **Target Viewer:** Robotics engineers, wearable designers, and AI hardware skeptics.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Always-on smart sensors will always kill wearable batteries in two hours."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What happens when every silicon pixel stays completely silent until the exact microsecond the world moves?"*

### Opening Option C: "Safety Without Surveillance Cameras" (Privacy & Dignity Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Inside 84-year-old Mrs. Sato's Kyoto bedroom: show what a cloud nanny-cam records (intrusive full video of her private life) vs. what a **2-milliwatt SynSense/Prophesee neuromorphic sensor** sees—pure, anonymous twinkling motion contours that vanish when she sleeps, yet instantly detect a slip on the rug with zero images ever stored or uploaded.
* **Target Viewer:** Families caring for aging parents, privacy advocates, hospitals, and civil-liberties skeptics.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Smart-home safety always requires creepy corporate surveillance cameras in our bedrooms."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"How do you build an eldercare guardian that physically cannot take a photograph?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Signature electric-cyan ($+$ brightness) and warm-magenta ($-$ brightness) microsecond **3D space-time spike ribbons** floating cleanly in pitch black—contrasted with blurred, washed-out RGB frames.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: "Photocopying the Whole Book 60 Times a Second vs. Sending Sticky Notes"
* **Visual Mechanics:** An exhausted clerk photocopies a 500-page encyclopedia every $16\text{ milliseconds}$ just because one comma changed on page 42 (60-fps RGB camera); beside him, an **Event Pixel** calmly hands over a single tiny sticky note reading *"Page 42, comma moved at 12:00:00.000050."*
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Makes event-based vision unforgettable to non-engineers in five seconds.
* **Data Ticker:** `60-fps RGB: Re-sends 95% Unchanged Pixels` vs. `Event Retina: Sends Only Microsecond Changes`.

### Metaphor 2: "The Sun-and-Cave Split Pupil (>120 dB)"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Standard camera forces one shutter speed across the whole frame (burning out the sun or blacking out the cave); on an Event Retina, 1,000,000 tiny independent pupils adjust their own sunglasses individually!
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Explains why $>120\text{ dB}$ logarithmic pixels conquer highway tunnel and headlight glare.
* **Data Ticker:** `Standard Camera Contrast: ~1,000:1 (60 dB)` $\rightarrow$ `Event Retina Contrast: >1,000,000:1 (>120 dB)`.

### Metaphor 3: "The Invisible Portrait (Privacy-by-Physics)"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Ask a person to sit still on a couch in front of an Event Retina—their face literally fades to **100% blank black** on the sensor feed! Only when someone falls does an anonymous stick-figure cascade appear.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Proves visually why neuromorphic healthcare/home sensors guarantee privacy by physics, not corporate promises.
* **Data Ticker:** `Identifiable Static Faces Captured: 0`.

### Metaphor 4: "The Clockwork Metronome vs. The Domino Reflex"
* **Visual Mechanics:** A synchronous GPU ticks a loud power-burning metronome constantly even in an empty room; a **Spiking Neural Chip** sits completely still at zero energy until one event domino taps the chain.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Connects asynchronous spiking logic to multi-year coin-cell battery life.
* **Data Ticker:** `GenX320 Wake-on-Event Standby: 36 Microwatts` | `Full SNN Reflex Inference: 1–10 Milliwatts`.
