# Storyboard & Production Guide: Weekend Explorer Swarms
**Pathway:** [`lidar-agent-citizen-archaeology`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Tell *both* halves of **Pietro Miotti's viral November 2026 Maya LiDAR story** with contagious wonder and fearless scientific honesty: how one curious person on vacation used public NASA laser data and **48 AI agents** to pinpoint a genuine ancient Maya pyramid complex in **72 hours**—and how the scholarly realization that it **rediscovered** a 2025 academic site missing from Mexico's public registry proved the technology's blind accuracy while inspiring a global shield against illegal rainforest bulldozers.

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "Hiram Bingham on a Laptop" (Viral Adventure Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Sunlit vacation table with an espresso cup beside a laptop. On screen, 35 meters of impenetrable green Campeche jungle canopy peel away like a silk curtain to reveal crisp geometric Maya terraces, a leveled plaza, and an **8–12 m oval pyramid**—analyzed simultaneously by **48 glowing AI agent cursors** ticking down a 72-hour clock.
* **Target Viewer:** Makers, technologists, history buffs, and curious weekend builders.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Real scientific exploration requires million-dollar grants and elite institutional credentials."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What happens when public NASA laser archives meet a 48-agent AI expedition team that never sleeps?"*

### Opening Option B: "The Blind Test Twist" (Scientific Integrity & Skeptic-Buster Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** Fast-cut documentary thriller: Miotti's Spanish report lands at Mexico's **INAH** ("Not on our public register!") $\rightarrow$ global headlines erupt $\rightarrow$ Maya archaeologists overlay **Estrada-Belli, Canuto, Šprajc & Fernández-Díaz's 2025 academic map**—and every single pyramid edge snaps into 1-to-1 alignment!
* **Target Viewer:** Scientists, journalists, and AI skeptics expecting an "AI hallucination" takedown.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"AI agents just hallucinate faces and fake ruins in random rocks."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"Why was 'rediscovering' a site missing from the government map actually the ultimate blind proof that AI citizen archaeology works?"*

### Opening Option C: "Faster Than the Bulldozer" (Indigenous Stewardship Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Split-screen aerial view over southern Mexico: on the left, an illegal logging bulldozer chews toward a forested limestone hill; on the right, Maya forestry guardian **Ixchel Balam** taps an encrypted LiDAR swarm alert that locks a legal **Heritage Protection Shield** around the hidden temple before noon.
* **Target Viewer:** Conservationists, policymakers, and cultural heritage advocates.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Mapping ancient sites online will just hand treasure maps to looters."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"How do coordinate-redacted AI swarms help Indigenous rangers protect thousands of hidden sites before chainsaws ever reach them?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Lush emerald rainforest canopy photography dissolving seamlessly into gold-and-obsidian bare-earth LiDAR sky-view reliefs, paired with warm human portraits of independent coders and Maya field archaeologists.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: The "Digital Silk Curtain Peel"
* **Visual Mechanics:** A photorealistic 3D rainforest block where a golden horizontal laser plane sweeps downward, dissolving green leaves into translucent mist while leaving crisp limestone pyramids, causeways, and reservoirs glowing like carved amber.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Demystifies how airborne LiDAR sees through 99% canopy cover without harming a single branch.
* **Data Ticker:** `Laser Pulses: 300,000+/sec` $\rightarrow$ `Tiny Canopy Gaps (<2%)` $\rightarrow$ `Sub-Meter Bare-Earth Relief`.

### Metaphor 2: The "4-Model Jury Against Pareidolia"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Four distinct colored lenses (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, Grok) inspect the same rugged limestone hill from four sun angles; natural hills fail 3 of 4 lenses, while artificial 90-degree Maya plazas light up all four simultaneously.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Shows how multi-family agent ensembles eliminate single-model visual hallucinations.
* **Data Ticker:** `1,200 km² Screened` $\rightarrow$ `99.9% Natural Karst Rejected` $\rightarrow$ `Blind Ground-Truth Match`.

### Metaphor 3: The "Two Maps That Never Spoke" Bridge
* **Visual Mechanics:** On the left sits a towering stack of locked English/Spanish academic PDFs; on the right sits a municipal bulldozer-permit map with blank white spaces; a glowing **Literature RAG Bridge** snaps the two together in real time.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Captures the deeper lesson of Miotti's INAH vs. 2025 paper twist—unmapped in government databases means unprotected on the ground!
* **Data Ticker:** `Academic PDF Silo + State Zoning Registry` = `Unified Sovereign Heritage Shield`.

### Metaphor 4: The "Scrambled Tile Safe" (Anti-Looting Ethics)
* **Visual Mechanics:** Public citizen-science screens display unlabeled, rotated elevation puzzle pieces with zero latitude/longitude; when an anomaly is confirmed, a cryptographic key unlocks coordinates exclusively inside INAH and Indigenous ranger tablets.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Reassures viewers that democratized AI discovery empowers protectors, never looters.
* **Data Ticker:** `Public Tile Coordinates: Stripped` | `Steward Access: Verified Indigenous & Ministry Teams Only`.

  1. *Independent AI Builder (Pietro Miotti archetype)* walking through the exhilaration of the 72-hour sweep and the humility of the scholarly rediscovery.

  3. *Indigenous Forest Guardian / INAH Conservator* showing how rapid remote mapping stops illegal land clearing in real time.
