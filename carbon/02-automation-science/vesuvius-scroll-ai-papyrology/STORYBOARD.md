# Storyboard & Production Guide: Reading Ashes Without Opening Them
**Pathway:** [`vesuvius-scroll-ai-papyrology`](REPORT.md) | **Pillar 2:** Embodied AI, Robotics & Self-Driving Science

> **Editorial & Visual Mandate:**
> Make viewers feel chills watching **black charcoal speak across two thousand years**: dramatize how Mount Vesuvius flash-baked the only surviving library of antiquity in 79 CE, why every physical touch shattered it, and how open-source student ML teams + synchrotron X-rays culminated in the **June 2026 full-scroll unwrapping of *PHerc. 1667***—rescuing a lost Stoic treatise linked to Chrysippus's school without disturbing a speck of ash.

---

## Part 1: Three Creative Openings

### Opening Option A: "The Burnt Log That Speaks Greek" (Pure Wonder Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Extreme macro shot of *PHerc. 1667*—a twisted, cracked, jet-black lump of charcoal under museum glass in Naples. Suddenly, the camera plunges *inside* the solid charcoal at 4-micron X-ray resolution, soaring through 180 fused spiral layers until glowing golden Greek letters (**ΑΡΙϹΤΟΚΡΕΩΝ** — *Aristocreon*) materialize across a flattened 1.5-meter scroll!
* **Target Viewer:** General audiences, history lovers, students, and documentary fans.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"AI only churns out synthetic slop—it never adds anything timeless to human culture."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"What if the greatest gift of 21st-century AI is giving humanity back the lost library of the ancient world?"*

### Opening Option B: "99% Lost—Until Now" (Civilizational Stakes Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:40):** A breathtaking infinite marble library representing all Greek and Roman literature—then 99 out of every 100 shelves vanish into darkness (83 of Sophocles' plays, Sappho's books, 700 rolls of Chrysippus). Cut to the buried tunnels beneath Herculaneum's **Villa of the Papyri**, where thousands of intact scrolls still wait under volcanic rock.
* **Target Viewer:** Humanities scholars, philanthropists, and cultural preservationists.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"What perished in the fall of Rome and the Library of Alexandria is gone forever."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"Now that we can read carbonized scrolls end-to-end without touching them, how much of classical antiquity is still waiting underground?"*

### Opening Option C: "The \$1M Discord Time Machine" (Open-Science Crowd Hook)
* **Visual Hook (0:00–0:35):** Fast-paced split screen: **Brent Seales** loading a 2,000-year-old scroll into the **ESRF particle accelerator** in France while 21-year-old **Luke Farritor** in a Nebraska dorm, **Youssef Nader** in Berlin, and **Julian Schilliger** in Zurich trade open-source neural weights on Discord at 3:00 AM.
* **Target Viewer:** Engineers, open-source builders, and science-funding reformers.
* **Cynic Reflex Countered:** *"Centuries-old academic mysteries only yield to slow, closed ivory-tower committees."*
* **Core Dramatic Question:** *"How did open-data micro-prizes let college students solve a 270-year archaeological cold case in thirty-six months?"*

### Visual Tone Summary
* **Camera Philosophy:** Tactile obsidian textures of volcanic ash and warm Pompeian fresco reds juxtaposed with luminous synchrotron X-ray cross-sections and golden uncial Greek typography.

## Part 3: Four Signature Visual & Infographic Metaphors

### Metaphor 1: The "Swiss-Roll Cinnamon Bun Cutaway"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Slice virtually through a twisted charcoal cylinder showing 180 tightly fused spiral wraps crushed into W-folds by volcanic mud; watch the topological tracer color each contiguous spiral sheet a distinct rainbow ribbon before gliding it flat without a single tear.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Makes 3D volumetric segmentation intuitive in five seconds to any viewer.
* **Data Ticker:** `180 Crushed Wraps` $\rightarrow$ `Bijective 3D Surface Mesh` $\rightarrow$ `1.5-Meter Flat Virtual Papyrus`.

### Metaphor 2: "Why the AI Cannot Hallucinate Letters"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Show the strict architectural wall: the 3D ink-detector processes tiny $64 \times 64 \times 64$ micron sub-cubes **smaller than a single Greek letter stroke**—it has zero language model vocabulary and doesn't even know Greek exists! Coherent Greek sentences *only* appear when thousands of independent micron predictions stitch together.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Instantly demolishes skeptic fears that the AI is "making up Greek text."
* **Data Ticker:** `Receptive Field: Sub-Letter Voxel Patch` + `Language Prior: Zero` = `100% Authentic Physical Ink`.

### Metaphor 3: The "99% Dark Library Re-Lit"
* **Visual Mechanics:** A vast circular hall of 1,000 dark bookshelves representing lost classical literature; as *PHerc. 172*, *PHerc. 1667*, and future Villa scrolls are scanned, warm golden lanterns reignite shelf after shelf of Stoic philosophy, tragedy, and history.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Connects a machine-learning breakthrough to civilizational memory and meaning.
* **Data Ticker:** `<1% of Classical Literature Survived` $\rightarrow$ `Hundreds of Intact Scrolls Ready to Scan`.

### Metaphor 4: The "Open-Bounty Relay Baton"
* **Visual Mechanics:** Instead of one isolated scholar working for 20 years, a glowing open-source code baton passes every 90 days—from EduceLab's synchrotron scan $\rightarrow$ Schilliger's sheet tracer $\rightarrow$ Nader & Farritor's ink detector $\rightarrow$ Naples papyrologists' translation.
* **Infographic Takeaway:** Demonstrates why staged open-science prizes outperform siloed grant monopolies.
* **Data Ticker:** `270 Years Unread` $\rightarrow$ `39 Months from Launch to First Complete Unopened Scroll`.
