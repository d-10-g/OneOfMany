# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: RIGHT TO REPAIR & MODULAR CIRCULARITY ([`right-to-repair-modular-circularity`](REPORT.md))
## *"Five Screws, Zero Glue, Thirty Years — How Vienna, Brussels, and Modular Engineers Ended Planned Obsolescence"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips filmmakers, industrial-design storytellers, and consumer advocates to contrast the absurdity of glued-shut e-waste mountains against the tactile satisfaction of **Framework & Fairphone 5-minute screwdriver upgrades**, **EU Digital Product Passports**, and **Austria's million-voucher *Reparaturbonus***.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "11-Year-Old Beats the \$1,500 Tech Giant in 4 Minutes" Challenge *(Kinetic, Fun, Viral)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen countdown timer (`04:00`). Left: a professional technician sweating with a heat gun, razor pry-bars, and solvent trying to extract a glued-in laptop battery before sparks fly. Right: 11-year-old Clara in Vienna calmly turns **five captive Torx screws** on her **Framework Laptop**, scans an internal QR code, snaps in a brand-new 2033 mainboard and battery with `CLICK-CLICK`, and boots up with `01:12` to spare!
* **Voiceover Core:** *"For fifteen years, tech monopolies swore they had to glue your \$1,500 laptop shut to keep it thin. Then engineers proved an eleven-year-old with one screwdriver can upgrade the whole computer in under three minutes."*
* **Best For:** YouTube/streaming openers, school STEM screenings, and consumer-rights campaigns.

### Option B: The "1924 Phoebus Lightbulb Conspiracy to 2026 Liberation" *(Historical Intrigue)*
* **Visual Hook:** Moody 1924 Geneva boardroom: executives sign the real historical **Phoebus Cartel** pact fining any factory whose lightbulbs lasted longer than 1,000 hours—cutting to a 62-million-tonne mountain of discarded smartphones and washing machines—then smash-cutting to a sunny Vienna cobbler and electronics repair shop ringing up voucher **#1,000,000** on Austria's **50% *Reparaturbonus***.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Planned obsolescence wasn't a conspiracy theory—it started in a 1924 Geneva cartel room, and for a century it quietly drained trillions from family budgets. In 2026, Europe, Vienna, and open hardware engineers broke the spell."*
* **Best For:** Feature documentaries, economic-history podcasts/videos, and keynote films.

### Option C: The "Farmer Locked Out of His Own Tractor at Harvest" *(Heartland, Bipartisan)*
* **Visual Hook:** Golden wheat field in Colorado at sunset as storm clouds gather; a combine harvester halts over a \$40 exhaust sensor because corporate software refuses an independent mechanic's wrench (`DEALER UNLOCK REQUIRED: $1,800 + 6-DAY WAIT`). Cut to bipartisan Right-to-Repair bills passing across Colorado, Minnesota, and Europe—handing farmers and families back their own keys.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"If you can't fix what you bought, you don't really own it—you're just renting it from a monopoly."*
* **Best For:** Rural-urban coalition screenings, statehouse briefings, and American heartland audiences.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE TEARDOWN

### 1. The "LEGO Brick vs. Glued Concrete Block" Appliance X-Ray
* **Visual Concept:** Side-by-side translucent X-ray: when one red gear wears out inside a glued concrete block, a wrecking ball smashes the whole cube; inside the modular chassis, two fingers lift out the single red cartridge and click in an upgraded gold gear in three seconds.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Crisp macro mechanical animation with magnetic snap cues modeled directly on Framework Expansion Cards.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes modular circularity feel sleek, futuristic, and common-sense.

### 2. The "Sticker-Covered Heirloom Laptop" (Ship of Theseus)
* **Visual Concept:** Time-lapse across 12 years of a child's bedroom desk: the beloved dented aluminum shell and travel stickers stay constant from middle school through university graduation while internal brains silently leapfrog three generations of compute.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Overhead locked-off match-dissolve across changing textbooks, posters, and sunlit seasons.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Replaces status-anxious throwaway consumerism with emotional attachment and craft pride.

### 3. The "Laser QR Passport Unfolding in Mid-Air"
* **Visual Concept:** Scanning a tiny laser-etched QR matrix on a 14-year-old espresso pump unfolds a floating iron-blue exploded blueprint, fair spare-part prices, and signed 3D sintering files.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Augmented-reality pull-out inside a warm Vienna neighborhood repair guild.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows how EU Digital Product Passports empower every local technician on Earth.

### 4. The "Bonus-Malus Eco Seesaw"
* **Visual Concept:** Glued `2/10` throwaway imports drop coins onto one end of a brass civic scale, automatically launching `50% Reparaturbonus` vouchers into citizens' phones at local `9/10` craft shops.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Playful tabletop motion graphic demonstrating zero-taxpayer-burden self-funding.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Resolves fiscal skepticism in eight seconds flat.
