# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: TAXING COMMONS, NOT SWEAT ([`compute-commons-taxation`](REPORT.md))
## *"Zero Tax on the First \$100,000 of Human Craft — How Land Value & Compute Rents Replaced Payroll Taxes"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard helps documentary filmmakers, visual explainers, and civic designers reveal the absurd 2026 tax penalty on human work—and dramatize how **Harrisburg, Taiwan, Singapore, and Estonia** proved we can untax human sweat, end vacant-lot speculation, and file taxes in 180 seconds.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Bakery vs. The Self-Checkout Kiosk" Tax Absurdity *(Punchy, Relatable, Eye-Opening)*
* **Visual Hook:** Inside Priya and Mateo's warm neighborhood bakery in 2026. On the left, they hire a young apprentice baker at \$50,000—a giant red stamp slams down: `31% PAYROLL & INCOME TAX PENALTY!`. On the right, a corporate chain installs an automated kiosk—a green stamp chimes: `<4% TAX AFTER WRITE-OFFS!`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In 2026, politicians gave speeches about saving jobs while our tax code quietly fined every small business 30 percent for hiring a human being—and taxed robots almost nothing. We didn't have a technology problem; we had an upside-down tax rule."*
* **Best For:** Main Street audiences, economic explainers, and small-business features.

### Option B: Tallinn, Estonia on April 15th — The 180-Second Miracle *(Delightful, Global Contrast)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen on April 15th. Left (`US 2026`): A stressed parent surrounded by shoeboxes of receipts at midnight, crying over a 40-page tax form. Right (`Tallinn, Estonia`): A teacher sipping coffee in a sunlit cafe taps her phone twice; a timer reads `02:14 — Tax Filing Complete!`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Americans spent 6.5 billion hours every year agonizing over tax paperwork—not because math is hard, but because we taxed every human paycheck instead of upstream commons. Estonia proved filing takes three minutes. Then we went one step further."*
* **Best For:** Viral social documentaries, civic tech audiences, and broad household viewers.

### Option C: "Try Mailing an Acre of Manhattan to the Cayman Islands" *(Witty, Intellectual, Georgist)*
* **Visual Hook:** Comedic CGI heist: A billionaire in a tuxedo tries to roll up a vacant city block in downtown Austin like a carpet and stuff it into a tiny envelope addressed to `P.O. Box 402, Cayman Islands`. It won't budge an inch.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"For decades, cynics said taxing wealth was impossible because billionaires move shell companies offshore. Nobel Laureate Joseph Stiglitz pointed out the obvious: nobody can hide an acre of prime urban land or a gigawatt AI power line in Bermuda."*
* **Best For:** Policy debates, TED-style talks, and fiscal reform campaigns.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. The "Unmovable Land Parcel vs. Paper Shell Company"
* **Visual Concept:** Paper tax-haven binders flutter away like moths toward offshore islands, while a glowing golden city block and a 500-MW substation stay anchored firmly into bedrock, feeding clean revenue directly into local schools and parks.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Ground-level architectural cutaway showing utility and land-registry meters operating silently with zero lawyers required.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shatters the cynical myth that "the rich will always dodge taxes" by shifting from paper accounting to physical reality.

### 2. "Lifting the Tax Weight Off the Baker's Shoulders"
* **Visual Concept:** A literal 30-pound iron vest labeled `Payroll & Income Tax` unbuckles from a nurse, a carpenter, and a young baker—instantly turning into a `+22% Take-Home Raise` while small-business hiring doors swing wide open.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Slow-motion warm natural lighting inside real workshops, classrooms, and kitchens.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Connects fiscal policy directly to everyday dignity and Main Street vitality.

### 3. The "Parking Lot Blooming Into Timber Homes" Time-Lapse
* **Visual Concept:** Under split-rate LVT, a cracked asphalt lot with chain-link fencing fast-forwards over 12 months into a lush, five-story Cross-Laminated Timber courtyard building with ground-floor cafes and rooftop trees.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Fixed-tripod architectural morph grounded in Harrisburg's real 88% blight reduction.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves good tax design physically heals broken cities.

### 4. The "Three-Minute Coffee Timer" (Tallinn e-Tax Standard)
* **Visual Concept:** An espresso shot pulls in real time (`28 seconds`) beside Mateo's smartphone as his pre-filled civic tax ledger verifies, totals `$0.00 Tax on <$100k Human Craft`, and completes before the crema settles.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Warm macro tabletop shot contrasting a dustbin full of shredded 1040 instruction booklets in the background.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Turns April 15th from a national trauma into a non-event.
