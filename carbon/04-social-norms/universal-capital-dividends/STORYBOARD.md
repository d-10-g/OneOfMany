# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: EVERYONE AN OWNER ([`universal-capital-dividends`](REPORT.md))
## *"From Selling Hours to Owning the Machines — Sovereign AI Funds & Universal Baby Bonds"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips filmmakers, motion designers, and civic storytellers to dismantle the fear that *"five AI companies will own everything while workers own nothing"*—viscerally illustrating how **Norway's \$1.8T Sovereign Fund**, **Alaska's Permanent Fund**, **Singapore's CPF**, and **Connecticut's Baby Bonds** combine to make every child and worker a compounding shareholder.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Lights-Out Robot Factory Flip" Opening *(Provocative, Kinetic, Skeptic-Shattering)*
* **Visual Hook:** Tracking shot gliding through a gleaming lights-out robotic gigafactory at midnight—zero human workers on the floor, articulated arms assembling solar tiles and server racks at blurring speed while a profit counter climbs. Suddenly the camera zooms *inside* a robot's translucent chest core, revealing thousands of glowing micro-ledger threads streaming outward into ordinary apartments, classrooms, and barber shops across America.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Everyone asks the same terrified question about AI: if robots do the work, won't five trillionaires own everything while ordinary families own nothing? Only if we keep 19th-century ownership rules. When we wired every citizen into sovereign compute trusts and birthright equity, every robot on this floor started working for every family in town."*
* **Best For:** Tech/AI documentaries, viral social explainers, and addressing automation anxiety head-on.

### Option B: Marcus's 21st Birthday in Hartford *(Warm, Intimate, Emotionally Grounded)*
* **Visual Hook:** Backyard string lights in Hartford, Connecticut, 2037. Retired freight driver Samuel Okafor hugs his 21-year-old son Marcus as Marcus opens his **CT-Commonwealth Birthright Account**—automatically seeded on the day he was born in the maternity ward—vesting at **\$94,600**, enough to buy his acoustic woodworking studio tools and co-own a sunlit timber townhome debt-free.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"As economist Darrick Hamilton proved, wealth inequality endures because half our kids start adulthood at zero—or buried in debt. Connecticut changed that rule at birth, and then we scaled it nationwide."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream human-interest films, family audiences, and civic leadership summits.

### Option C: The "Four Proven Places on Earth" Map Opening *(Pragmatic, Bipartisan, Undeniable)*
* **Visual Hook:** Crisp 3D globe lighting up four real places in 2026: **Oslo, Norway** (`$320,000 invested per citizen`), **Anchorage, Alaska** (`Universal cash dividends since 1982`), **Singapore** (`90% homeownership via CPF/Temasek`), and **Hartford, Connecticut** (`1st automatic US Baby Bonds`).
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Making everyone a capital owner isn't a utopian theory—four very different governments from snowy conservative Alaska to Norway and Singapore already proved every piece of the math."*
* **Best For:** Policy makers, institutional investors, and cross-partisan town halls.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. The "21-Year Golden Snowball" ($t = 0$ Compounding)
* **Visual Concept:** A baseball-sized golden sphere (`$7,500` seeded in the maternity ward) rolls steadily across twenty-one changing seasons, picking up luminous rings of reinvested AI, solar, and biotech dividends until it arrives at Marcus's doorstep at age 21 as a solid `$94,600` foundation boulder.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Continuous side-tracking shot passing childhood milestones (first bicycle at age 6, high-school math class at age 16, studio keys at age 21).
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes the exponential curve of $A = P(1+r)^t$ unforgettable to viewers who were never taught how compounding builds generational freedom.

### 2. "Translucent Citizen Robots" (Same Side of the Ledger)
* **Visual Concept:** Inside an intimidating autonomous logistics and solar foundry, the camera triggers an X-ray shimmer—revealing thousands of ordinary citizen shareholder portraits (teachers, freight drivers, nurses, retirees) woven into the machines' revenue streams.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Macro pull-back from a humanoid robot's chest core following glowing dividend threads directly into neighborhood smartphones.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Instantly flips technological dread ("that robot is replacing us") into civic ownership pride ("that robot works for our family").

### 3. The "Crystalline Fiduciary Firewall"
* **Visual Concept:** A transparent, unbreakable glass wall separates partisan Capitol Hill podiums from citizen equity vaults, while families freely select ultra-low-fee (`0.04%`) private index managers on the citizen side.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Crisp isometric architectural diagram modeled on the \$900B+ US Federal Thrift Savings Plan and Norway's ethical sovereign fund.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Reassures free-market skeptics in five seconds that universal ownership means *more* private shareholders, never politicized state control.

### 4. "Three Aqueducts Feeding One Family Reservoir"
* **Visual Concept:** Three steady golden channels—(1) At-Birth Baby Bond, (2) Portable 10% Superannuation, and (3) Citizen Compute Trust Dividend—flowing into a household reservoir alongside human craft income.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Warm water-table visualization showing how the family basin stays brimming even when a parent takes six months off to care for a newborn or learn a new craft.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Visualizes multi-engine economic security without bureaucratic welfare stigma.
