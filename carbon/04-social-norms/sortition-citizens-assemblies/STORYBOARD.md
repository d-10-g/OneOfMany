# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: CITIZEN JURIES & SORTITION ([`sortition-citizens-assemblies`](REPORT.md))
## *"125 Strangers, Zero Lobbyists — How Civic Lotteries Break the Deadlocks Politicians Are Afraid to Touch"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips documentary directors, civic educators, and motion designers to capture the emotional electricity of **Stratified Sortition Citizens' Assemblies**—from **Dublin, Ostbelgien, and Paris** to **Stanford Deliberative Polls**—showing ordinary truckers, nurses, and grandparents forging 80%+ consensus when campaign cash and cable-TV soundbites leave the room.

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Golden Envelope in the Rural Mailbox" Opening *(Intimate, Cinematic, Character-Led)*
* **Visual Hook:** Frosty dawn in rural Ohio. Freight driver Dale Whittaker pulls a thick cream envelope stamped `OFFICIAL CIVIC ASSEMBLY SUMMONS — PAID STIPEND $320/DAY` from his mailbox. He scoffs: *"Politicians never listen to guys like me."* Smash-cut to four weekends later: Dale stands arm-in-arm with an urban pediatric nurse as state lawmakers pass their 84%-backed clean-grid royalty bill into law.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"We trust twelve random citizens in a jury box to decide life and liberty—yet for two centuries, we forgot we could use that exact same superpower to break political gridlock without a single lobbyist in the room."*
* **Best For:** Character-driven documentaries, heartland town halls, and streaming series openers.

### Option B: The "2,400-Year-Old Marble Machine (*Kleroterion*)" Opening *(Historical Wonder, Tactile)*
* **Visual Hook:** Close-up inside the Agora Museum in Athens on a carved marble slab pierced with rows of slots—an ancient Athenian *kleroterion* allotment machine—dropping bronze-and-white dice to pick everyday bakers and shipwrights for the Council. The marble slots morph seamlessly into open-source cryptographic stratification software in East Belgium and Paris.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"When ancient Athenians invented democracy, they didn't think elections were democratic—they thought elections favored the rich and famous. For true representation, they used a lottery. Twenty-four centuries later, Ireland, Belgium, and Paris brought it back—and it works."*
* **Best For:** Educational series, museum films, and constitutional-reform keynotes.

### Option C: The "Tuesday 2:00 PM Empty Microphone vs. The Full Circle" Contrast *(Witty, Relatable)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen: Left shows a dreary 2:00 PM city zoning meeting—three corporate lobbyists in \$2,000 suits and five angry retirees shouting at an exhausted councilmember while nurses and parents are stuck at work. Right shows a sunlit Saturday **Paris Citizens' Assembly**—100 paid neighbors of every age and zip code laughing and drafting real city law over coffee while free on-site childcare hums next door.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Most 'public input' is broken by design—it only hears whoever has a paid afternoon off or a lobbyist on retainer. Sortition invites the whole city to the table."*
* **Best For:** Urbanist channels, municipal reform campaigns, and youth civic workshops.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE DOCUMENTARY COMPANION

### 1. The "Soundbite Megaphone vs. The Round Table of Eight"
* **Visual Concept:** Two gladiators shouting through distorted megaphones across a chasm morphs into a warm circular oak table of eight strangers passing a talking-piece while an impartial engineer projects live cost-benefit sliders on the table surface.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Slow orbital dolly around Table 7 as body language shifts from crossed arms on Saturday morning to collaborative sketching by Sunday afternoon.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows physically how architecture and incentives—not human malice—drive polarization or consensus.

### 2. The "Political Lightning Rod" Shield
* **Visual Concept:** Partisan lightning bolts (`Lobbyist Threats`, `Primary Attack Ads`) strike harmlessly against a glowing civic dome labeled `84% Citizen Jury Supermajority`, allowing elected lawmakers underneath to sign generational reforms safely.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Wide capitol rotunda shot showing rival party legislators stepping into the calm light beneath the citizen mandate.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Explains why smart legislators welcome sortition rather than fearing it.

### 3. The "Ancient Marble *Kleroterion* to Cryptographic Lottery"
* **Visual Concept:** Bronze-and-white allotment dice dropping down a 2,400-year-old Athenian marble stone transform seamlessly into glowing demographic bars matching nurses, renters, rural truckers, and young parents within $\pm 1\%$ of the census.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Macro match-cut from ancient carved Greek stone slots to open-source Sortition Foundation audit code.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Connects timeless democratic heritage with modern statistical fairness.

### 4. The "Voter Pamphlet Truth Inoculation" (Oregon CIR)
* **Visual Concept:** Deceptive \$50M glossy attack flyers blow away like confetti when a family opens Page 1 of their official state voter guide to read the plain-English **24-Citizen Fact Statement**.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Kitchen-table close-up highlighting `Agreed by 20 of 24 Citizen Panelists` stamps.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes viewers want a Citizens' Initiative Review in their own state before the very next election.
