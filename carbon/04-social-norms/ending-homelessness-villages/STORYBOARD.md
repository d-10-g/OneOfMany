# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: ZERO UNSHELTERED NEIGHBORS ([`ending-homelessness-villages`](REPORT.md))
## *"From \$75,000 Street Sweeps to Sunlit Porches — How Helsinki, Houston & Austin Proved the 30-Month Cure"*

> **Production Purpose:** This high-empathy, fiercely practical storyboard equips documentary directors, municipal leaders, and visual explainers to end despair over urban tent encampments—showing through **Finland's Y-Foundation**, **Houston's 63% reduction**, and **Austin's *Community First! Village*** how cities achieve **Zero Unsheltered Neighbors in 30 Months** for one-third the cost of the status quo.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "Winter in Helsinki at -15°C: Where Are the Tent Encampments?" *(Stark Global Reveal)*
* **Visual Hook:** Snowy dusk walk through **Helsinki's** central train station, parks, and waterfront underpasses—clean, warm, peaceful, zero sidewalk tents. Cut inside a sunlit studio apartment four blocks away where a formerly unhoused man brews coffee behind his own locked front door while **Juha Kaakinen** (architect of Finland's Housing First miracle) explains the ledger: `Long-Term Homelessness Down >80% | Saves €15,000/Person/Year`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Walk across Helsinki in the middle of winter and you will notice something astonishing to American eyes: virtually zero people sleeping on the streets. Not because Finland sweeps poor people out of sight—but because Finland realized giving someone a permanent door costs fifteen thousand euros *less* than cycling them through emergency rooms."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream documentaries, mayors/governors, and shattering "nothing works" fatalism.

### Option B: Saturday Morning Inside Austin's *Community First! Village* *(Warm, Transformative, Human)*
* **Visual Hook:** Golden Texas morning sunlight across **51+ lush acres in Austin**: colorful architect-designed micro-cottages with wide porches, vegetable gardens, chickens, a blacksmith forge, and a pottery kiln. **Marcus** pulls glazed stoneware mugs from the kiln as founder **Alan Graham** greets neighbors by name. Graphic pops up: `86%+ Permanent Housing Retention`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In Austin, Texas, Alan Graham proved the deepest truth about chronic homelessness: housing alone doesn't cure catastrophic loneliness—community does. Inside Community First! Village, 86 percent of formerly chronically unhoused neighbors stay housed for good."*
* **Best For:** Human-centered films, faith/civic coalitions, and overcoming neighborhood siting fears.

### Option C: The "\$75,000 Burned on Concrete vs. \$24,000 Sunlit Cure" Ledger *(Fiscal Shock-to-Clarity)*
* **Visual Hook:** Overhead split-screen: Left (`2026 Freeway Underpass`): Ambulance sirens, police sweep trucks, and ER bills pile up to a towering crimson bar: **`$75,000 / Year Burned — Still Unhoused`**. Right (`30-Day Modular Dignity Cottage`): A crane places a \$22,000 solar cedar cottage onto landscaped ground; total annual cost lights up green: **`$24,000 / Year — Housed, Sober & Working`**.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In 2026, cities weren't too stingy to end street homelessness—we were paying seventy-five thousand dollars a year per person to keep them miserable on concrete."*
* **Best For:** Fiscal conservatives, taxpayer groups, and municipal budget hearings.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. "The Brass Deadbolt Click" (Sound & Tactile Anchor)
* **Visual Concept:** Extreme macro shot of a solid brass deadbolt sliding smoothly into a warm cedar doorframe—cutting instantly from roaring highway traffic noise outside to profound, peaceful acoustic silence inside.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** 2.5-second total audio drop at the exact click of the lock, letting the audience physically feel nervous-system safety.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Explains Housing First more powerfully than a thousand policy papers.

### 2. "Three Tangled Knots Untangling Into Three Sunlit Paths"
* **Visual Concept:** A dark, knotted rope (`Street Crisis`) gently separates into three distinct colored ribbons—Gold (`Track 1: Economic Rent Shock`), Emerald (`Track 2: Addiction Recovery`), and Sky Blue (`Track 3: Restorative Neuro-Care`)—each leading to its matched architectural home.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Clean overhead motion graphic resolving partisan "housing vs. treatment" fights.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows how precision compassion beats both neglect and criminalization.

### 3. "200 Scattered Puzzle Pieces Locking Into Houston's Single Screen"
* **Visual Concept:** Two hundred disconnected paper clipboards float chaotically until they snap magnetically into one glowing live tablet map showing `Available Cottage #14 — Ready in 22 Minutes`.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Crisp kinetic UI snap modeled on Houston's *Way Home* command center.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves accountability and speed matter as much as funding.

### 4. "From Wet Concrete Underpass to Glazed Stoneware Kiln"
* **Visual Concept:** Match-cut from rain dripping onto grey freeway concrete in 2025 to warm, glowing orange kiln light warming Marcus's hands as he lifts handmade ceramic coffee mugs stamped `Made at Evergreen Village` for neighborhood cafés.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Intimate 35mm close-up celebrating restored craftsmanship and earned dignity.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Embodies Alan Graham's insight that community and meaningful contribution complete the cure.
