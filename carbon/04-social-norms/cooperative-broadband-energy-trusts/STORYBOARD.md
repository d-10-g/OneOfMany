# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: COMMUNITY FIBER & MICROGRID CO-OPS ([`cooperative-broadband-energy-trusts`](REPORT.md))
## *"How a Tennessee Railroad Town Built Internet 250× Faster Than Wall Street — And Gave Every Kid Free Fiber"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips documentary filmmakers, rural-urban coalition builders, and motion designers to tell the electrifying underdog triumph of **Chattanooga's 25-Gbps EPB**, **Longmont's #1-rated NextLight**, **Utah's Open-Access UTOPIA**, and **Europe's 2,200+ REScoop citizen energy co-ops**.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "David vs. Goliath in Tennessee" Underdog Triumph *(Stirring, Bi-Partisan, Iconic)*
* **Visual Hook:** Archival shots of 1970s Chattanooga—once dubbed the dirtiest industrial air in America—transitioning to sleek glowing glass fibers strung beside neighborhood power poles against Lookout Mountain. Giant telecom lobbyists file four lawsuits to stop the town; Chattanooga wins every single one, flips the switch on **25,000-megabit community fiber**, and generates **\$2.7 billion** in local boomtown wealth.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Big cable monopolies spent millions telling Tennessee lawmakers a mid-sized town could never run high-tech internet. Chattanooga built it anyway—delivering twenty-five-gigabit speeds faster than Tokyo or Manhattan, cutting blackouts in half, and wiring twenty-eight thousand low-income schoolchildren for zero dollars."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream feature documentaries, heartland town halls, and viral openers.

### Option B: The "0.4-Second Storm Switch" Living Room Scene *(Visceral, Immediate, Relatable)*
* **Visual Hook:** Violent thunderstorm outside an East Chattanooga window—a falling oak snaps a feeder line in a shower of sparks. Slow-motion zoom into a pole-mounted **EPB IntelliRupter** communicating at light speed over community fiber: `FAULT ISOLATED IN 0.4 SECONDS`. Inside, a teenager's robotics stream and his mother's telehealth monitor don't even blink.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"When the town owns both the electric grid and the fiber-optic glass, smart switches heal storm outages before your coffee maker resets its clock."*
* **Best For:** Climate-resilience films, engineering showcases, and local-news explainers.

### Option C: The "Promotional Rate Runaround vs. Longmont's \$50 Lock" *(Witty, Cathartic)*
* **Visual Hook:** Comedic split-screen: Left shows a frazzled dad on minute `94` of customer-service hold music as his monopoly cable bill jumps from `\$59` to `\$134/mo`. Right shows his sister in **Longmont, Colorado**, smiling at a one-line **NextLight** municipal bill reading the exact same flat **\$49.95** for symmetrical gigabit fiber ten years straight!
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Why is Longmont, Colorado's municipal NextLight rated the number-one ISP in America? Because when the subscribers own the utility, nobody invents junk fees to trick themselves."*
* **Best For:** Consumer-advocacy shorts, municipal referendum campaigns, and social media.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE CALCULATOR

### 1. The "Public Highway vs. Private Tollbooth Driveway" (Ammon / UTOPIA Open Access)
* **Visual Concept:** Shows the absurdity of a private company owning the asphalt in front of your driveway and charging \$130 to pull out, transforming into neutral **Municipal Glass Asphalt** where 15 delivery vans (private ISPs) compete cheerfully for \$45/mo.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Isometric neighborhood pull-back as tollgates sink into the ground and glowing cyan fiber lines light up every porch equally.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Bridges free-market conservatives and civic progressives instantly.

### 2. The "Boomerang Dollar" Utility Bill
* **Visual Concept:** Every monthly \$58 payment arcs down to the neighborhood substation, strengthens storm switches, wires a classmate's home via EdConnect, and boomerangs back as Main Street jobs and solar dividends.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Golden light-trail animation contrasting Wall Street capital leakage against Chattanooga's audited \$2.7B local loop.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes cooperative capital retention tangible and inspiring.

### 3. The "Five-Minute Laser Swap" (50-Year Glass Permanence)
* **Visual Concept:** Underground glass fiber stays untouched beneath oak roots for half a century while a technician snaps in palm-sized optical cards taking a whole neighborhood from `1 Gbps` to `10 Gbps` to `25 Gbps` to `100 Gbps`.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Split underground/central-office macro lens highlighting pulses of pure multi-wavelength light.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves community fiber is generational infrastructure, never a depreciating gadget.

### 4. The "0.4-Second Self-Healing Storm Web"
* **Visual Concept:** When a storm limb snaps one glowing grid strand, fiber-linked IntelliRupter switches reroute electrons around the break before a kitchen chandelier can even dim.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** High-speed 1,000-fps storm sequence paired with a real-time millisecond counter (`0.40s`).
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Viscerally links community broadband to physical family safety.
