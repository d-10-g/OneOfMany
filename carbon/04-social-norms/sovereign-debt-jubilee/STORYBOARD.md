# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: DEFUSING THE HOUSEHOLD DEBT TRAP
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`sovereign-debt-jubilee`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard translates the empirical triumphs and human warmth of `REPORT.md` into a 4-minute documentary-style visual film. Instead of abstract financial jargon, it takes viewers from an anxious first-of-the-month kitchen table in Toledo to Copenhagen's 200-year transparent bond halls, North Carolina's historic medical debt wipeout, and a sunlit 2031 neighborhood freed from 29% usury forever.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "The Yellow Envelope in Toledo" (Intimate Family Liberation)
* **Hook:** Open on Marcus and Elena's refrigerator top in 2026—a shoebox stuffed with unopened red hospital collection notices and a 28.4% credit-card statement. Cut to the morning a bright **Undue Medical Debt** yellow envelope arrives from the City of Toledo, wiping out \$11,400 in C-section bills for \$115 of city funds, unlocking their path to a **1.5% Sovereign Homestead** home.
* **Visual Arc:** Intimate domestic claustrophobia dissolving into wide porch sunlight, moving boxes in a mass-timber bungalow, and Saturday morning youth soccer.
* **Best For:** Broad mainstream audiences, viral human-interest storytelling, and municipal reform campaigns.

### Angle B: "One Penny on the Dollar, Two Centuries in Denmark" (The Empirical Anti-Cynic Proof)
* **Hook:** Hold a single copper penny up to the camera beside a \$1.00 hospital bill. Ask the viewer: *"What if every piece of a debt-free society wasn't a utopian theory, but already working in Ohio, North Carolina, Australia, and Denmark today?"* Tour the real-world receipts rapid-fire.
* **Visual Arc:** Crisp global documentary travelogue leaping from Cook County and Raleigh to Copenhagen's brick courtyards and Melbourne university campuses.
* **Best For:** Skeptical viewers, civic leaders, journalists, and policy explainers.

### Angle C: "Why Banks Actually Get Safer Without Usury" (Systemic Financial Clarity)
* **Hook:** Contrast the chaotic 2023 Silicon Valley Bank interest-rate implosion with **Denmark's Realkredit covered-bond exchange**, which hasn't suffered a systemic mortgage bailout since **1797**. Show how par-value Homestead bonds and Australia's HECS system protect both families and savers as AI lowers physical living costs.
* **Visual Arc:** Clean kinetic ledger diagrams showing 1-to-1 pass-through covered bonds replacing fragile speculative debt bubbles.
* **Best For:** Economists, credit-union boards, state treasurers, and institutional investors.

## 3. Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Motif Bank

* **The Shoebox vs. The Bright Yellow Envelope:**
  * *Visual:* Contrast the heavy, shameful stack of red collection notices hidden atop the kitchen fridge with the real, iconic bright yellow *Undue Medical Debt* forgiveness letter—"Paid in Full, No Strings Attached."
  * *Audio Cue:* Muffled anxious refrigerator hum cutting to crisp morning birdsong and tearing paper as the bill hits recycling.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `$11,400 Hospital Collection Balance` $\rightarrow$ `Bought & Abolished for $115 (1 Cent / Dollar)`.
* **The One-Penny Lever:**
  * *Visual:* Physical tabletop choreography where a single copper coin tips an oak balance scale holding \$1.00 of medical collection paper—making secondary-market debt math unforgettable in three seconds.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `$10,000 Civic Fund = $1,000,000 Neighbor Medical Debt Erased`.
* **Copenhagen Brick & Toledo Mass-Timber:**
  * *Visual:* Pair warm sunlit footage of Danish *Realkredit* family courtyards (est. 1797) with modern American mass-timber infill homes (`04-social-norms`) to show 200-year institutional continuity rather than sci-fi speculation.
  * *Audio Cue:* Bicycle bells on cobblestones blending seamlessly into nail-free timber joinery clicking into place.
* **The Two First-of-the-Month Calendars:**
  * *Visual:* Split-screen of a wall calendar flipping to `OCTOBER 1`: in 2026, four red auto-draft alerts drain 52% of family pay; in 2031, a single calm `$740 Homestead Bond` draft leaves a thriving family surplus.

## 4. Cinematography, Sound Design & Cross-Pillar Production Bible

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **Act I (2026 Debt Squeeze):** Tight 50mm framing under cool overhead tungsten-fluorescent shadows; harsh red overdue ink and cold smartphone banking screens.
* **Act II (Global Empirical Tour):** Crisp, natural sunlit documentary realism across Cook County, Raleigh, Copenhagen, and Melbourne—warm brick, clean archival bond ledgers, and authentic civic halls.
* **Act III (2031 Homestead Morning):** Expansive 35mm golden-hour warmth—honey-toned mass timber, linen curtains, steaming coffee mugs, and vibrant green neighborhood soccer fields.

### Sound Design & Musical Score Arc
* **Opening Tension:** Suffocating, rapid mechanical adding-machine ticks layered with vibrating unknown-caller collection buzzes on a kitchen counter.
* **The Breakthrough Turn (Scene 3):** Adding-machine clatter stops dead on the single metallic clink of one copper penny landing on oak—followed by warm cello, acoustic guitar, and light percussion.
* **Diegetic Joy Anchors:** Front-door key turning in a family's first owned timber home, espresso steam, and six-year-olds cheering after a Saturday morning goal.
