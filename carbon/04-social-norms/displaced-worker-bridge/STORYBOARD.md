# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: RESCUING DISPLACED WORKERS ([`displaced-worker-bridge`](REPORT.md))
## *"Why Danes Don't Panic About AI — The 90% Wage-Bridge & 90-Day Craft Pivot"*

> **Production Purpose:** This urgent-triage storyboard equips documentary directors, labor storytellers, and policy communicators to answer the sharpest 2026–2029 kitchen-table fear—*"What happens to mid-career coders, call-center leads, paralegals, and truckers laid off next Tuesday?"*—using **Denmark's 90% Flexicurity Golden Triangle**, **Sweden's Job Security Councils**, and **San Antonio's Project QUEST**.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "Same Friday AI Layoff: Ohio 2025 vs. Copenhagen (and Ohio 2028)" *(High-Stakes Split-Screen)*
* **Visual Hook:** Two 44-year-old freight dispatchers receive the exact same Friday afternoon video call: *"AI routing agents have automated our division."* Left frame (`Legacy US 2025`): Red COBRA letter (`$1,950/mo`), crashed unemployment website (`$420/wk max`), sleepless 3:00 AM stare at the ceiling. Right frame (`Copenhagen / Upgraded Columbus 2028`): **David Kowalski's** phone calmly confirms `90% Net Paycheck Continuity Locked | Family Healthcare Portable | Monday Grid-Guild Cohort Ready`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"As Nobel Laureate Daron Acemoglu reminds us, machines never decide whether automation ruins families—institutions do. In Copenhagen, losing a job to technology has meant 90 percent pay continuity and rapid retraining for thirty years. In 2027, we brought that exact bridge home."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream documentaries, prime-time news features, and mid-career working families.

### Option B: "Why 75% of Danish Workers Cheer Workplace Robots" *(Surprising Global Paradox)*
* **Visual Hook:** Inside a sleek Danish maritime and clean-energy port where dockworkers and engineers high-five as autonomous cranes come online. Poll graphic pops up: `Workers Viewing Automation Positively: US 32% vs. Denmark & Sweden 76%+`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Ask an American worker about AI and you hear fear of losing their house. Ask a Dane or a Swede and three out of four welcome the machines. Their secret isn't cultural magic—it's a three-sided safety architecture called Flexicurity."*
* **Best For:** Economics/policy series, business roundtables, and labor-management summits.

### Option C: From Ghost-Job Résumé Hell to Commissioning a Hospital Microgrid *(Triumphant Human Arc)*
* **Visual Hook:** Close-up on David Kowalski deleting `Resume_Final_v47.pdf` from his laptop, lacing up composite-toe boots, and walking onto a sunlit Columbus hospital roof alongside 18 fellow former dispatchers and QA coders to bring a 4-MW solar-battery microgrid online.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"We didn't need displaced dispatchers and coders to rot in isolated bedrooms sending résumés to bots—we needed their systems brains outdoors, rebuilding our power grid and sunlit towns."*
* **Best For:** Future-of-work stories, workforce guild recruitment, and hopeful civic shorts.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. "Broken Rope Bridge vs. Illuminated Nordic Truss Bridge"
* **Visual Concept:** In 2026, workers crossing between industries face a rotting rope bridge missing planks (`$380/wk UI`, `COBRA Cliff`) over a dark canyon; in 2028, a wide, sunlit steel-and-timber pedestrian bridge (`90% Pay + Portable Health + 90-Day Guild`) carries whole cohorts safely across in stride.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Sweeping drone-style lateral tracking shot moving seamlessly with a walking cohort.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Viscerally shows why institutional architecture determines whether change feels exciting or terrifying.

### 2. "Skill-Prism Refraction" (Veteran Experience -> New Craft)
* **Visual Concept:** A beam of white light labeled `20 Years Dispatch / Paralegal / Support Mastery` hits a crystal prism and refracts into vibrant spectrum beams powering microgrids, mass-timber cranes, civic mediation, and longevity studios.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Clean macro optical animation set inside real industrial training bays.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Honors mid-career workers' accumulated wisdom instead of treating them as "obsolete."

### 3. "The 70% Escrow Padlock Against Scam Schools"
* **Visual Concept:** A gleaming golden vault door on guild tuition stays locked until the graduate badges into Month 6 of their new \$85,000 career—automatically starving predatory diploma mills.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Crisp mechanical lock-click synchronized with David's first 6-month milestone badge.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Convinces skeptics immediately that public retraining dollars cannot be wasted.

### 4. "Deleting `Resume_v47.pdf` for Good"
* **Visual Concept:** Extreme close-up of a cursor dragging 47 unanswered ghost-job PDF résumés into the trash bin, closing the laptop lid in a dark bedroom, and opening a heavy sunlit workshop rolling door into a bustling 18-person guild bay.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** High-contrast light bloom flooding the frame as the workshop door rolls upward.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Captures the visceral relief of stepping out of algorithmic rejection into real human teamwork.
