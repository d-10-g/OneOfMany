# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: ERASING BILLIONS FOR PENNIES ([`medical-debt-abolition-pools`](REPORT.md))
## *"The Yellow Envelope Miracle — How Two Former Debt Collectors, Cities & North Carolina's 99 Hospitals Abolished \$24B+ in Medical Debt"*

> **Production Purpose:** This deeply moving, non-partisan storyboard equips documentary filmmakers, city councilors, faith coalitions, and state health leaders to shatter fatalism around medical bankruptcy—showing how **1-cent-on-the-dollar civic buyout pools** and **North Carolina's \$4 billion / 99-hospital Medicaid compact** rescue millions of working families right now while upstream zero-billing care ends new medical debt at the source.

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "Two Former Debt Collectors Walk Into a Secondary Debt Market" *(Insider Heist-for-Good Hook)*
* **Visual Hook:** Moody close-up of veteran debt-collection executives **Craig Antico** and **Jerry Ashton** laying a hospital receivables spreadsheet onto a wooden table: a **\$10,000 emergency surgery bill** marked down for sale to predatory collectors for **\$78.00**. They slide a single crisp **\$100 bill** across the table—and instead of mailing a wage-garnishment summons, drop a bright **sunny yellow Undue Medical Debt envelope** into a mailbox.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"For decades, hospitals quietly sold unpaid medical bills to debt buyers for less than a penny on the dollar—who then hounded sick families for the full hundred cents. Until two insiders left the collection industry, bought those exact portfolios for pennies, and started setting billions of dollars on fire."*
* **Best For:** Feature documentaries, viral investigative explainers, and cross-partisan civic audiences.

### Option B: "Tuesday Morning on a Toledo Front Porch: Opening the Yellow Envelope" *(Pure Human Catharsis)*
* **Visual Hook:** Quiet kitchen light in **Toledo, Ohio**: bakery manager **Elena Morales** stares at a stack of unopened red-ink hospital notices beside her healthy 3-year-old son's highchair, then nervously opens a bright yellow city-partnered **Undue Medical Debt** envelope. Her hand covers her mouth as tears of relief spill down her cheeks: `Balance Paid in Full: $19,400 -> $0.00 | Cost to City Pool: ~$160`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Elena didn't apply to a government agency or hire a bankruptcy lawyer. Her city spent less than two hundred dollars—and lifted nineteen thousand dollars of neonatal ICU terror off her family forever."*
* **Best For:** Broadcast human-interest profiles, municipal town halls, and congregation debt drives.

### Option C: "99 Out of 99 Hospitals in North Carolina Say Yes" *(Systemic Policy Breakthrough)*
* **Visual Hook:** Map of **North Carolina** lighting up hospital by hospital from Blue Ridge mountain towns to coastal counties until **all 99 acute-care hospitals** glow emerald green: `CMS-Approved HASP Compact: $4,000,000,000 Erased Back to 2014 | 2,000,000 Residents Freed`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Think state politics is too polarized to touch healthcare debt? Watch what happened when North Carolina offered all ninety-nine hospitals enhanced Medicaid stability in exchange for wiping out four billion dollars of working families' medical bills."*
* **Best For:** Governors, state Medicaid directors, hospital executives, and policy summits.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. "One Copper Penny Lifting a 100-Pound Iron Anchor"
* **Visual Concept:** Clean physical balance scale: a massive rusted iron anchor stamped `Hospital Collection Bill: $100` sits on a kitchen table until a single gleaming copper penny (`1¢ Civic Pool`) drops onto the opposite lever and effortlessly launches the anchor into thin air.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Tactile tabletop macro cinematography with crisp counterweight physics.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Instantly teaches every viewer and city councilor the 100-to-1 secondary-market math.

### 2. "The Bright Yellow Envelope Cutting Through Red Ink"
* **Visual Concept:** Overhead flat-lay of dozens of menacing crimson `FINAL COLLECTION NOTICE` envelopes burying a kitchen table until one warm, sunlit **yellow Undue Medical Debt envelope** lands at the center and sweeps every red notice to clean white wood.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Stop-motion paper choreography with a warm acoustic chime.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Transforms financial shame into collective civic grace.

### 3. "Closing Both the Drain and the Faucet"
* **Visual Concept:** Two-part animated diagram: `Civic Pools + NC HASP` drain the flooded basement of past debt, while `200ms Charity API + $75 Direct Care` shut off the broken billing faucet overhead.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Smooth isometric architectural cutaway.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows skeptics why upstream prevention and retroactive jubilee belong together.
