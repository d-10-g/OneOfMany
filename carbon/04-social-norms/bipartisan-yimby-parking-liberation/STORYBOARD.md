# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: UNFREEZING THE 1970s STREET ([`bipartisan-yimby-parking-liberation`](REPORT.md))
## *"We Didn't Lose Affordable Neighborhoods to Human Nature — We Outlawed Them in 1974, and Left & Right Are Unfreezing Them Together"*

> **Production Purpose:** This 4:15 documentary storyboard unmasks one of modern life's biggest optical illusions—showing viewers that \$2,500 starter rents and scorching six-lane strip malls were engineered by arbitrary 1950–1978 parking mandates, hallway rules, and duplex bans, then celebrating the real-world **-18% real rent drop in Austin**, **~1% six-year rent miracle in Minneapolis**, and **Republican–Environmentalist "Montana Miracle"** already bringing starter homes back.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "The Renewal Letter That Actually Went Down" *(Warm, Relatable, Surprising)*
* **Visual Hook:** South Austin kitchen counter, October 2025. NICU nurse and fiddle player **Elena Reyes** opens a lease-renewal envelope bracing for another \$200 hike—then blinks in disbelief as the camera pushes into the printed line: **New Monthly Rent: -\$310/Month Lower + 6 Weeks Free**. Cut outside to a baking 18-car asphalt lot transforming into nine shaded, live-oak mass-timber courtyard townhomes.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Everyone told Elena that once city rents shoot up, they never come down. Then Austin repealed mandatory parking minimums, legalized three starter homes per lot, and built fifty thousand apartments—and landlords started competing for nurses again."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream kitchen-table explainers, young renter audiences, and viral social reels.

### Option B: "Why Your Grandparents' Favorite Street Is Literally Illegal Today" *(Visual History Reveal)*
* **Visual Hook:** Warm sunlit walk down a beloved 1925 Main Street—corner bakery with apartments upstairs, shaded porch duplexes, a courtyard fourplex, kids on bikes. Suddenly red holographic `ILLEGAL (1968 CODE)` stamps slam onto every single building: `VIOLATION: NO 24-CAR ASPHALT LOT`, `VIOLATION: SINGLE STAIRWELL`, `VIOLATION: DUPLEX BAN`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"We think sprawling strip malls and twenty-five-hundred-dollar studios are free-market capitalism. In truth, between 1950 and 1978, cities quietly made almost every walkable, affordable street in America against the law."*
* **Best For:** Urban-design mini-docs, town-hall screenings, and cross-generational storytelling.

### Option C: "The Cowboy & The Conservationist Shake Hands in Helena" *(Cross-Tribal Hope)*
* **Visual Hook:** Crisp snowy morning outside the Montana State Capitol. A Carhartt-jacketed Kalispell carpenter, a free-market **Frontier Institute** researcher, a **Habitat for Humanity** volunteer, and a trout-stream conservationist sign **SB 323 & SB 528** together—cutting to **Caleb Miller** craning an \$84,000 mass-timber cottage into his backyard for his widowed mom.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In a polarized country, look at what united Texas progressives, Minnesota labor unions, and Montana conservatives: letting families build starter homes and backyard cottages on their own land instead of paving ranchland."*
* **Best For:** Bipartisan policy summits, state legislators, and heartland broadcasts.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR & B-ROLL MOTIF BANK

* **1. The "Asphalt Ice Cube Melting Into Live Oaks" Time-Lapse:**
  * *Visual:* Gray 1974 parking-lot concrete literally thaws like winter ice, revealing rich permeable brick lanes, kids chalk-drawing under oak shade, and warm honey-toned mass-timber porches.
  * *Audio Cue:* Harsh highway tire roar softening into acoustic South Austin fiddle, laughter, bicycles clicking, and evening cicadas.
* **2. The "Invisible \$65,000 Concrete Ghost SUV":**
  * *Visual:* Translucent concrete phantom SUV hovering inside a young nurse's living room, eating \$350 bills out of her grocery jar—until a single council gavel tap (`PARKING MANDATE REPEALED`) pops it into sunlit third-bedroom space.
  * *Audio Cue:* Crisp stone-crumble dissolve followed by warm morning birdsong through an opening casement window.
* **3. The "100 Tiny Moving Vans" Domino Chain:**
  * *Visual:* Tactile wooden tabletop model of a city where opening one mass-timber courtyard building sets off a cheerful cascade of moving vans freeing older bungalows across four working-class zip codes.
  * *Audio Cue:* Playful marimba notes stepping down in pitch like falling rent numbers.
* **4. The Single-Stair Window Bloom:**
  * *Visual:* Isometric cutaway watching a dark hotel hallway shrink into a compact central stair core as triple-exposure windows and cross-breezes flood every family apartment with morning sunlight.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `68% Hallway Box` → `88% Sunlit Family Courtyard`.
