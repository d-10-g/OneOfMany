# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: ZERO-CLIFF SAFETY NETS ([`zero-cliff-safety-nets`](REPORT.md))
## *"Never Punished for Working — How We Abolished the 90% Welfare Cliff and Cut Child Poverty in Half"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips directors, animators, and civic storytellers to expose one of modern policy's cruelest hidden bugs—the **75%–95% marginal welfare cliff**—and show through **Stockton's SEED pilot**, the **2021 Child Tax Credit**, and **Finland/Kenya trials** how a Zero-Cliff Universal Floor unlocks work, pride, and family peace.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "Turning Down a Raise in Tears" *(Raw, Human, Instantly Infuriating—Then Joyful)*
* **Visual Hook:** Inside a pediatric dental clinic in 2026: a boss warmly offers a hardworking single mom a \$2.50/hour raise. Instead of celebrating, she stares at a crumpled notepad showing that earning \$3,000 more will cost her **\$8,500** in lost childcare and Medicaid—forcing her to whisper, *"I can't afford the raise."* Fast-forward to Stockton in 2031: **Danielle Brooks** gets the exact same promotion, checks her smooth green **Zero-Cliff Glide Curve**, and hugs her clinic director with joy.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In 2026, billionaires paid a top marginal tax rate of 37 percent—while single moms working two jobs faced an invisible 90 percent penalty if they accepted a raise. We called it a safety net, but it operated like a trap. Here is how we turned it into a launchpad."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream documentaries, emotional viral shorts, and cross-partisan audiences.

### Option B: "When Milton Friedman and Martin Luther King Jr. Agreed" *(Surprising Historical Hook)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split archival portrait in crisp black-and-white: free-market Nobel Laureate **Milton Friedman** at a chalkboard in 1968 beside **Dr. Martin Luther King Jr.** writing *Where Do We Go from Here?*. Their two quotes converge into one clean golden mathematical curve: `A Guaranteed Floor With Zero Welfare Cliffs`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Milton Friedman and Martin Luther King Jr. disagreed on almost everything in politics—except one big idea: fire the paternalistic welfare bureaucracy, abolish anti-work cliffs, and build a dignified cash floor under every citizen."*
* **Best For:** Policy thinkers, bipartisan civic events, and economics explainers.

### Option C: The "Stockton SEED Surprise" Data Reveal *(Empirical Myth-Buster)*
* **Visual Hook:** Street-level morning sunlight in Stockton, California. Mayor **Michael Tubbs** launches the SEED trial giving \$500/month with zero strings attached. Pundits on TV shout: *"People will stop working!"* Then the real UPenn research bar chart animates upward: `Full-Time Employment: 28% -> 40% (+12 Points!)`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Cynics swore that trusting working families with unconditional cash would make them quit their jobs. When Stockton actually ran the randomized trial, full-time employment jumped more than twice as fast as the control group—because you can't interview for a better job when your car won't start."*
* **Best For:** Evidence-driven journalism, TED-style talks, and debunking cynicism.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. "Sheer Red Cliff vs. Sunlit Golden Ramp"
* **Visual Concept:** A visceral side-by-side 3D path: on the left, a parent climbing with two kids hits an overhang labeled `$34,000 Cutoff` that drops straight down; on the right, a continuous gentle ramp rises smoothly from `$18,000 Floor` all the way into middle-class prosperity without a single crack.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Smooth tracking shot moving alongside the parent's footsteps as every step upward adds glowing green height.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes "effective marginal tax rates" instantly understandable to a 12-year-old or a Senator.

### 2. "Sticky Spiderweb vs. Woven Trampoline"
* **Visual Concept:** Legacy welfare is shown as a tangled grey web of 80 bureaucratic strings that trap your ankles if you try to jump higher; it transforms into a taut, resilient golden trampoline beneath every neighborhood.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Upward kinetic bounce showing entrepreneurs, students, and displaced workers leaping toward higher skills without fear of hitting concrete.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Captures why security *increases* ambition and full-time employment.

### 3. "Clearing the Fog of Scarcity (-13 IQ Restored)"
* **Visual Concept:** In Act I, intrusive red pop-ups (`Recertification Due!`, `Asset Limit Exceeded!`, `Overdraft $35!`) crowd 60% of the screen frame; in Act III, the frame clears completely into wide-angle, crystal-clear cinema light.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Shallow-to-deep depth-of-field transition centered on a parent's relaxed face.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Lets the viewer *feel* the psychological liberation of unconditional stability.

### 4. "Payday-Loan Neon Going Dark at Sunrise"
* **Visual Concept:** A strip-mall row of flashing predatory `400% APR CASH ADVANCE` neon signs flickers off permanently as dawn breaks and neighbors' phones chime quietly with their Friday 6:00 AM Zero-Cliff smoothing floor.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Golden sunrise dolly shot transitioning from shuttered loan-shark storefronts to busy hardware stores, community colleges, and pediatric clinics.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows how predictable weekly liquidity starves extractive poverty industries overnight.
