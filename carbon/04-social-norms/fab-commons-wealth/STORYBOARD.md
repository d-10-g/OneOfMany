# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: DEMONETIZING THE ESSENTIALS ([`fab-commons-wealth`](REPORT.md))
## *"Palaces for the People — When a Modest Paycheck Buys a 2026 Millionaire's Life"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips filmmakers, civic architects, and visual explainers to show how lived inequality evaporates peacefully when clean energy, robotic timber homes, outpatient cures, and open-weight AI slash the five scarce bottlenecks by 80%—anchored by **Helsinki's Oodi Central Library** scaled to every postal code.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Andy Warhol Smartphone & Penicillin" Insight *(Clever, Eye-Opening, Philosophical)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen of a billionaire boarding a private jet and a community-college student on a park bench: both pull the exact same smartphone from their pocket, look up the exact same Wikipedia page, and take the exact same \$4 antibiotic. Then the camera pans to **five locked golden doors** behind the billionaire in 2026: `Housing`, `Concierge Medicine`, `Private Tutors`, `Elite Lawyers`, `Clean Energy`. Suddenly, clean technology and civic commons blow all five doors wide open for the student.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Today, a billionaire can't buy a better penicillin pill or Wikipedia page than a teenager. Inequality only hurts where essentials stay scarce. When we collapsed the cost of housing, medicine, tutoring, legal defense, and energy by eighty percent, a modest income started buying a 2026 millionaire's life."*
* **Best For:** Broad documentaries, TED-style explainers, and reframing wealth debates beyond zero-sum anger.

### Option B: Inside Helsinki's Oodi "Palace for the People" *(Warm, Architectural, Joyful)*
* **Visual Hook:** Sweeping steadicam glide through snow-dusted **Helsinki** into the glowing curved spruce timber atrium of **Oodi Central Library**. Inside, a retired bus mechanic 3D-prints a replacement gear beside a teenage girl recording a violin track in a soundproof studio and an immigrant mother laser-cutting patterns—all with a free public library card.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Architect Tommi Laitio built Helsinki's Oodi Library around one radical norm: public spaces shouldn't feel like dingy charity for the poor—they should be palaces for the people where every citizen owns free access to the finest tools on Earth."*
* **Best For:** Design/urbanism series, civic inspiration films, and municipal town halls.

### Option C: Malik & Maya's Saturday in Minneapolis (2034) *(Intimate Intergenerational Story)*
* **Visual Hook:** Retired mechanic **Malik Carter** (\$42k pension) checks his clear coronary scan on his watch (`Outpatient Peptide Tune-Up: $12`) before walking three blocks with his granddaughter **Maya** to their neighborhood **Oodi-North Fab-Commons** to build a custom telescope mount on a public CNC mill.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In 2026, Malik couldn't afford elite tutors, lawyers, or concierge doctors for his family. By 2034, nobody needed millions in the bank to have all three."*
* **Best For:** Family audiences, PBS/streaming features, and human-centered storytelling.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK, SOUND DESIGN & INTERACTIVE COMPANION

### 1. "Five Golden Locks Clicking Open"
* **Visual Concept:** Five ornate brass padlocks (`Shelter`, `Longevity`, `Mastery`, `Justice`, `Clean Power`) that gated upper-class security in 2026 click open one by one and melt into a warm public bridge open to every citizen.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Tactile macro metalwork animation transitioning into warm Nordic spruce architecture.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Reframes equality around concrete lived capabilities rather than abstract resentment.

### 2. "80 Cluttered Garages vs. 1 Sunlit Guild Wall"
* **Visual Concept:** Overhead cutaway of 80 dark suburban garages stuffed with tangled cords and broken cheap drills consolidating into one gleaming, sunlit pegboard of precision tools, cargo e-bikes, and audio gear three blocks away.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Wes Anderson-style symmetrical overhead snap.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes circular abundance feel luxurious rather than ascetic.

### 3. "The Parliament-to-Library Mirror" (Helsinki's Civic Equalizer)
* **Visual Concept:** Wide eye-level shot from Helsinki's Kansalaistori square showing the steps of Oodi Central Library standing at the exact same eye-level height as the columns of Finland's Parliament House across the plaza.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Slow 180-degree pan at blue hour as glowing timber warmth meets civic stone.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Visually cements Tommi Laitio's thesis that knowledge and creation belong on the highest pedestal in a democracy.

### 4. "The 13-Minute Landfill Drill vs. The Immortal Guild Tool"
* **Visual Concept:** Stop-motion sequence of 60 cheap plastic drills gathering dust for ten years before tumbling into a dump—rewinding and fusing into a single gleaming brushed-steel precision drill checked out joyfully by 60 neighbors over a decade.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Tactile tabletop stop-motion with crisp mechanical sound effects.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Connects circular ecology with effortless everyday luxury.
