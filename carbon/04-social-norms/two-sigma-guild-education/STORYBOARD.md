# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: 2-SIGMA AI MASTERY & HUMAN GUILDS
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`two-sigma-guild-education`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard brings the joyful paradox of `REPORT.md` to life in a vivid 4-minute film: how **two focused morning hours of Socratic 1-on-1 AI tutoring** liberate children from seven hours of factory-desk boredom and nightly homework, unlocking **four phone-free afternoon hours of outdoor adventure, woodworking, theater, robotics, and human mentorship**.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "The 10:45 AM Ship's Bell" (A Day in Mateo's Shoes)
* **Hook:** Open on 11-year-old Mateo in 2026 hiding his math worksheet in shame while a classroom clock crawls endlessly at 1:52 PM. Match-cut to Oak Creek Commons School in 2031: Mateo mastering algebra via skateboard physics at 9:30 AM—and then at **10:45 AM**, a brass ship's bell rings, every tablet snaps shut, and 200 kids burst outside into timber barns, creeks, and theater stages with zero homework tonight.
* **Visual Arc:** Fluorescent desk isolation transforming into sunny morning focus, muddy creek boots, sawdust workshops, and an unhurried family taco dinner.
* **Best For:** Parents, students, teachers, and mainstream audiences craving hope for childhood.

### Angle B: "Solving Benjamin Bloom's 40-Year Mystery" (Empirical Science Story)
* **Hook:** Open on archival 1984 University of Chicago bell-curve chalkboard graphics as a narrator explains **Benjamin Bloom's 2-Sigma Problem**: how 1-on-1 mastery lifts an average 50th-percentile student to the **98th percentile**, and how Socratic AI (*Khanmigo*, *Alpha School* top-1% growth data) plus **Finland's play-rich afternoons** finally cracked the 40-year cost barrier for every public-school child.
* **Visual Arc:** Crisp data storytelling weaving 1984 psychometrics with live footage from Brownsville, Austin, Helsinki, and Zurich guilds.
* **Best For:** School boards, education researchers, superintendents, and science-minded skeptics.

### Angle C: "Ms. Alvarez Gets Her Sunday Nights Back" (The Teacher Renaissance)
* **Hook:** Follow fifth-grade teacher Ms. Alvarez—who nearly quit teaching in 2026 under 18 hours of weekend grading and test-prep mandates—as Socratic AI takes over repetitive worksheet drilling, freeing her to sit beside struggling nine-year-old Priya with wooden blocks in the morning and direct the **Watershed & Culinary Guild** in the afternoon.
* **Visual Arc:** From lonely Sunday-night red-pen burnout to joyful 1-on-1 human connection and respected \$110,000 Master Guide leadership.
* **Best For:** Educators, teacher unions, and community mentorship recruiters.

## 3. Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Motif Bank

* **The Brass Bell & Clicking Tablet Covers:**
  * *Visual:* A rhythmic, satisfying 3-second montage of thirty linen-covered tablets snapping shut at 10:45 AM, immediately followed by pine curls flying off a hand plane and rubber boots splashing into a sunlit creek.
  * *Audio Cue:* Crisp magnetic tablet clicks punctuated by one resonant brass ship's bell and an explosion of outdoor children's laughter.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `10:45 AM: Core Mastery Complete` $\rightarrow$ `Zero Screens Until Tomorrow`.
* **Conveyor Belt vs. Skateboard Ramp:**
  * *Visual:* Contrast the rigid 1893 row-and-column desk grid with Mateo rolling a physical maple skateboard up a wooden ramp in the afternoon that matches the exact slope ratio equation he solved at 9:30 AM.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `Morning Equation` $\rightarrow$ `Afternoon Timber Craft`.
* **The Empty Sunday Red-Pen Basket:**
  * *Visual:* Ms. Alvarez sweeping a tangled bundle of red grading pens and bubble-test scantrons into a storage bin and walking out into Sunday afternoon sunshine with her own kids.
  * *Audio Cue:* Quiet sigh of relief transitioning into warm cello and backyard wind chimes.
* **The Cedar Smartphone Cubby:**
  * *Visual:* Close-up of a sunlit cedar entryway wall holding simple family voice-watches while kids trade jokes eye-to-eye in the hallway—zero hunched necks or anxious scrolling.

## 4. Cinematography, Sound Design & Cross-Pillar Production Bible

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **Act I (1893 Factory Classroom):** Static, locked-off symmetry under cool grey-green fluorescent tubes; pale paper stacks and ticking wall clocks.
* **Act II Morning (2-Sigma Studio):** Soft, airy Scandinavian daylight streaming across birch tables, tactile wooden math blocks, and calm e-ink/stylus displays.
* **Act II Afternoon & Act III (Human Guilds & Home):** Kinetic 35mm handheld warmth—golden cedar shavings, emerald creek reflections, rich kitchen tomatoes, and amber twilight porch glow.

### Sound Design & Musical Score Arc
* **Opening:** Monotonous wall-clock tick, muffled fluorescent buzz, and anxious smartphone vibration pings under desks.
* **The 10:45 AM Pivot:** One clear brass ship's bell opens a joyful, propulsive acoustic score—plucked upright bass, marimba, fiddle, and handclaps.
* **Diegetic Craft Texture:** Hand saws singing through cedar, sizzling garlic in the Culinary Guild, water rushing over creek stones, and acoustic guitar chords on Mateo's evening porch.
