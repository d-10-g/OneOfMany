# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: COMMUNITY LAND TRUSTS ([`community-land-trusts-permanence`](REPORT.md))
## *"When the New Park Opens, Nobody Gets Pushed Out — How Community Land Trusts Lock In Forever Affordability AND Family Wealth"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips documentary directors, urban-policy animators, and housing storytellers to visualize the emotional and mathematical brilliance of **Community Land Trusts (CLTs)**—honoring **1969 Georgia civil-rights founders Shirley & Charles Sherrod**, **Burlington's Champlain Housing Trust**, **Houston CLT**, **London CLT**, and **Brussels *CLTB***.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Two Keys Handed Forward" Moving-Day Opening *(Warm, Tearjerking, Hopeful)*
* **Visual Hook:** Sunlit porch of a mass-timber townhouse two blocks from a gleaming Houston light-rail stop. Pediatric therapist Carla Mendez hugs 27-year-old music teacher Devon Brooks, handing him the front-door keys. An animated split-ledger floats gently: Carla walks away with a **`$54,200 Equity Check`** toward her growing family's next home, while Devon buys this exact 3-bedroom house for **`$188,000`** in a neighborhood where speculative flippers charge \$490,000!
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In most booming cities, a new train station means the teachers and nurses get priced out within three years. On a Community Land Trust block, one smart rule lets Carla build fifty-four thousand dollars in family wealth—and pass the exact same affordable starter home forward forever."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream human-interest features, municipal housing summits, and social explainers.

### Option B: 1969 Albany, Georgia — The Civil-Rights Invention Nobody Taught in Economics *(Deep Roots, Powerful)*
* **Visual Hook:** Rich 1969 archival photographs in Lee County, Georgia: **Charles and Shirley Sherrod** standing on 5,735 acres of red clay pinewood at **New Communities Inc.**, drafting the world's first Community Land Trust charter so Black farming families could never again be evicted for registering to vote—cutting seamlessly to modern passive-solar CLT apartments in Brussels and Burlington.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"The smartest antidote to modern urban gentrification wasn't invented in a Wall Street lab—it was born in 1969 Georgia out of the American Civil Rights Movement, and today it anchors thousands of homes from Vermont and Texas to London and Brussels."*
* **Best For:** Documentary films, museum exhibitions, and history-of-ideas broadcasts.

### Option C: The "Split-Deed Architectural Lift" Visual Metaphor *(Crisp, 10-Second Clarity)*
* **Visual Hook:** Clean isometric 3D model of a cozy timber house sitting on green earth. Speculative red flames (`+240% Land Speculation`) lick around neighboring lots—until the CLT house gently separates two inches above a glowing golden bedrock tablet labeled `99-YEAR DEMOCRATIC LAND TRUST`. The red flames vanish; the timber house stays cool, calm, and family-owned.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Buildings don't cause housing bubbles—speculating on dirt does. Separate the home you own from the community land underneath, and housing panic ends overnight."*
* **Best For:** Fast-paced urbanist explainers, YouTube/TikTok shorts, and zoning town halls.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE EQUITY CALCULATOR

### 1. The "One Seed That Feeds Four Generations" Orchard Tree
* **Visual Concept:** Contrasts an annual cut bouquet (15-year developer tax credit that withers and lands in the trash) against planting a deep-rooted **CLT Peach Tree** whose initial \$75,000 root investment yields \$50,000 nest-egg harvests for Family 1, Family 2, Family 3, and Family 4 across thirty years.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Time-lapse seasons cycling across a sunlit porch as four successive young families hang their nameplates on the same timber front door.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Convinces fiscal conservatives and housing advocates alike why recycled equity beats expiring subsidies 5-to-1.

### 2. The "Split-Deed Shock Absorber"
* **Visual Concept:** When speculative earthquakes shake surrounding market prices up and down, the golden 99-year ground-lease spring beneath the CLT home absorbs the shock—keeping monthly payments flat and foreclosures 10× lower.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Side-elevation cutaway contrasting 2008 subprime collapse next door against calm, steady Champlain Housing Trust equity bars.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes legal ground-lease mechanics visually effortless.

### 3. The "Pre-Subway Land Anchor"
* **Visual Concept:** Before a new blue transit line is drawn on the city map, golden CLT anchors drop onto 25% of station-area parcels—so when the train arrives, nurses and teachers ride the wave instead of getting washed out.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Overhead map sweep across Houston, London, and Brussels transit corridors.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Cures urban fear of green upgrades and new infrastructure.

### 4. The "Tripartite Compass Table (`1/3 + 1/3 + 1/3`)"
* **Visual Concept:** Three interlocking wooden arcs—current CLT homeowners, surrounding block renters, and public civic stewards—locking into one unbreakable ring.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Top-down shot of a lively neighborhood trust meeting welcoming the newest teacher buyer.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Visualizes democratic permanence across generations.
