# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: ALGORITHMS THAT REWARD COMMON GROUND
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`bridging-consensus-trust`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard turns `REPORT.md` into an energizing, myth-shattering 4-minute documentary film. It shows skeptical viewers why online polarization was an **engineering bug in 2010s engagement math—not human nature**—and takes them inside **Taiwan's vTaiwan/Polis miracle**, **C2PA camera silicon**, **Ireland's Citizens' Assemblies**, and a snowy Michigan town turning a screaming zoning war into an **89% consensus ballpark and housing win**.

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "No Reply Button for Outrage" (Audrey Tang & The Taiwan Miracle)
* **Hook:** Open on split-screen chaos of cable-news shouting and toxic comment threads—then cut to Taipei civic hacker and Digital Minister **Audrey Tang** smiling calmly beside a live **Polis** cluster map. Reveal how deleting the dunking reply button and ranking **cross-group agreement** solved 26+ explosive national fights with **>80% legislative consensus**.
* **Visual Arc:** Neon digital noise resolving into luminous, organic cluster constellations converging on shared green consensus bridges.
* **Best For:** Broad digital audiences, tech builders, journalists, and democracy reformers.

### Angle B: "Coach Darnell's Proposal #184" (The Riverbend Town Turnaround)
* **Hook:** Open on a 2026 Riverbend city-council meeting descending into shouting over a riverfront rail yard. Fast-forward to 2030: Little League coach **Darnell** typing a nuanced three-part compromise into **Riverbend Polis** from his kitchen table, watching opposing clusters light up **89% green**, and throwing out the first pitch at the new riverfront diamond sixteen months later.
* **Visual Arc:** Local heartland story moving from town-hall exhaustion to cinnamon-roll citizen roundtables and Friday-night Little League lights.
* **Best For:** Municipal leaders, community groups, school boards, and everyday voters tired of partisan fights.

### Angle C: "Shared Truth Without Censorship Boards" (C2PA + Bridging Math)
* **Hook:** Show an AI deepfake crisis clip going viral in three seconds—then zoom inside a **Sony/Qualcomm C2PA camera sensor** signing real photons at the lens, paired with **Community Notes matrix factorization** where left and right raters *must agree* before context appears. Prove we can defeat deepfakes and disinfo with **cryptography and math, zero censorship boards**.
* **Visual Arc:** Macro silicon camera optics and crisp mathematical animations explaining trust without central gatekeepers.
* **Best For:** Civil libertarians, engineers, newsrooms, and free-expression advocates.

## 3. Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Motif Bank

* **The Podium Microphone Line vs. The Round Table:**
  * *Visual:* Contrast the lonely, adversarial metal podium microphone (angled up at stern officials on a raised dais) with five warm wooden round tables at eye level holding coffee carafes, cinnamon rolls, and neighborhood maps.
  * *Audio Cue:* Harsh feedback screech of a town-hall mic cutting cleanly into warm coffee pouring and calm multi-voice conversation.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `3-Minute Soundbite Rage` $\rightarrow$ `89% Bridged Neighbor Compact`.
* **The Live Polis Cluster Bridge:**
  * *Visual:* Signature motion graphic where two drifting islands of red and blue dots pause as a single golden citizen statement arcs between them, pulling hundreds of dots into a luminous emerald common-ground center.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `Zero Reply-Dunking` \| `Ranked by Cross-Camp Agreement`.
* **The C2PA Photon Seal:**
  * *Visual:* Golden rays of real sunlight passing through glass camera elements and crystallizing into an unbroken hexagonal watermark—contrasted with synthetic deepfake pixels dissolving like sand.

  * *Visual:* Commuter looking up from a warped, angry social feed to see real neighbors sharing an umbrella at the bus stop.
  * *Audio Cue:* Distorted digital static snapping cleanly into gentle rainfall and a friendly *"Here, share mine."*
  * *On-Screen Label:* `Perceived Hostility: 2.5x Reality` $\rightarrow$ `Real Neighbor Decency`.
