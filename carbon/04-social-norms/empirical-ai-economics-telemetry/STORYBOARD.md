# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: REAL-TIME AI ECONOMICS ([`empirical-ai-economics-telemetry`](REPORT.md))
## *"Inside the 70% Miracle — How Privacy Clean Rooms and Open Deliverables Revealed What AI Really Does for Humanity"*

> **Production Purpose:** This cinematic storyboard guides documentary filmmakers, data-visualization studios, and civic storytellers in translating **Dr. Ronnie Chatterji's NBER w34255 telemetry breakthrough**, the open-sourced **`GDPval`** deliverable benchmark, and **Global South independent replications** into a vivid human story of unmeasured household flourishing and smart workforce adaptation.

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "70% Invisible Iceberg" Opening *(Eye-Opening, Data-Driven, Global)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen montage at 8:00 PM across four time zones: a mother in Pune translating her toddler's blood test into Marathi; a Nigerian agronomy student mastering statistics; a retired carpenter in Ohio preparing questions for his cardiologist; a paralegal in Chicago formatting a brief. Only the fourth screen lights up a tiny brass `GDP` meter (`30% Work`); the first three glow radiant gold (`>70% Non-Work Life Flourishing — $0 in Legacy GDP`).
* **Voiceover Core:** *"For two years, pundits argued over whether AI was replacing office clerks. Then economist Ronnie Chatterji and colleagues analyzed anonymized patterns across ten percent of the world's adults—without reading a single private message—and discovered a shock: over seventy percent of consumer AI use wasn't corporate work at all. It was families upgrading their daily lives in ways traditional GDP couldn't even see."*
* **Best For:** Flagship streaming documentaries, keynote openers, and broad public explainers.

### Option B: The "14-Year Veteran Taste Test (`GDPval`)" Opening *(Tactile, Witty, Grounded)*
* **Visual Hook:** A sunlit workshop table where a veteran structural nurse, a 15-year commercial lawyer, and a senior financial analyst sit before two unlabelled folders—Folder A and Folder B—containing complete, multi-file real-world deliverables (spreadsheets, care plans, slide decks). No multiple-choice bubble sheets in sight—an old `MMLU Scantron` goes into a recycling bin.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Passing a college multiple-choice quiz doesn't run a hospital or balance a city budget. With GDPval, economists open-sourced 220 real-world deliverables across 44 occupations—turning AI hype into an honest, blinded taste test."*
* **Best For:** Workforce-development summits, university classrooms, and labor-policy features.

### Option C: The "Glass Clean Room With Frosted Prompts" Metaphor *(Visual Clarity on Privacy)*
* **Visual Hook:** Millions of glowing personal envelopes stream toward a crystalline vault. Inside the vault, no human sits—automated prism beams sort envelopes purely by color-coded intent (`Asking 49%`, `Doing 40%`, `Expressing 11%`) while incinerating the text inside, releasing clean aggregate bars only when at least 100 envelopes match.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"How do you measure the biggest economic shift since electricity without spying on a single human soul? Welcome inside the cryptographic Data Clean Room."*
* **Best For:** Digital-rights audiences, tech-policy briefings, and museum interactives.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE DOCUMENTARY COMPANION

### 1. The "Frosted Prism Vault" (Privacy-Preserving Automated Classifiers)
* **Visual Concept:** Translucent glass prism where personal messages enter wrapped in cryptographic seals; light refracts out into clean 24-color macro rainbows (`Practical Guidance`, `Health Literacy`, `Writing`, `Tutoring`) only when 100+ beams converge, leaving zero readable text behind.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Macro tracking shot through the prism wall as raw text dissolves into luminous particles and exits solely as aggregate NBER histogram bars.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Instantly demystifies how NBER w34255 measured 10% of humanity while protecting every individual's secrets.

### 2. The "Scantron Bubble Sheet vs. The Architect's Blueprint (`GDPval`)"
* **Visual Concept:** Morphing animation turning a flimsy `#2 Pencil Multiple-Choice Quiz` into a multi-tabbed financial model, clinical nursing handoff, and CAD tolerances reviewed blind by 14-year master practitioners.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Overhead tabletop whip-pan across 44 occupational craft stations spanning all nine major GDP sectors.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows why real deliverable benchmarks empower workers and educators far better than sensationalized IQ claims.

### 3. The "70% Underwater Household Miracle"
* **Visual Concept:** Above the waterline floats a small grey barge (`30% Paid Workplace Prompts`); beneath glows a vibrant coral reef of families translating pediatric charts, acing university math, and diagnosing farm crops (`>70% Non-Work Consumer Surplus`).
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Smooth vertical crane plunge beneath the surface across Pune, Lagos, São Paulo, and Durham homes.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes viewers feel the human warmth hidden behind Stanford $\text{GDP-B}$ and Global South telemetry numbers.

### 4. The "30-Day Guild Compass"
* **Visual Concept:** Instead of a weathervane spinning wildly in media hype, an apprentice-hall brass compass wired to live `GDPval` win-tie curves rotates calmly toward high-judgment human crafts.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Push-in on early-career apprentices stepping confidently from routine spreadsheet prep into courtroom and clinical leadership.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Turns AI workplace anxiety into concrete, rapid personal agency.
