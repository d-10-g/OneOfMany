# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: SOCIAL PRESCRIBING & BELONGING ([`social-prescribing-loneliness-cure`](REPORT.md))
## *"Rx: One Woodworking Shed & Tuesday Choir — How Doctors and Link Workers Cured the 15-Cigarette-a-Day Loneliness Crisis"*

> **Production Purpose:** This deeply human storyboard equips documentary directors, healthcare communicators, and civic storytellers to film the quiet revolution of **Social Prescribing Link Workers**—spanning **Compassionate Frome's 14% drop in townwide emergency admissions**, **3,500+ UK NHS Link Workers**, **Australian Men's Sheds**, and **Singapore's Wellbeing Coordinators**.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Prescription Pad Surprise" Opening *(Intimate, Emotional, Instantly Memorable)*
* **Visual Hook:** Extreme close-up on a doctor's hand poised above a prescription pad opposite 74-year-old widower Arthur, whose coat pocket already rattles with four pill bottles. Instead of writing `Zolpidem 10mg`, the doctor writes: **`Rx: Salford Canal Men's Shed — Tuesday 9:30 AM (With Nadia)`**. Cut to Arthur three weeks later, laughing with sawdust on his sweater as he teaches a 17-year-old how to wire a wooden rowing skiff.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Chronic loneliness is as lethal to the human heart as smoking fifteen cigarettes a day—and no pill on Earth cures an empty kitchen. Across Britain, Canada, and Singapore, doctors started prescribing something radical: people who need you back."*
* **Best For:** Mainstream documentaries, family viewers, and keynote emotional hooks.

### Option B: The Market Town That Emptied Its Emergency Ward (*Compassionate Frome*) *(Intriguing Medical Mystery)*
* **Visual Hook:** Misty morning aerial over cobblestone **Frome, Somerset (pop. 28,000)**. On a hospital chart, emergency admissions across every neighboring county climb steeply upward (`+28.5%`)—except one glowing green line for Frome plunging **down `-14%`**! How? Cut to Frome's hairdressers, taxi drivers, and café baristas wearing small `Community Connector` badges beside GP Dr. Helen Kingston.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Between 2013 and 2017, one English town pulled off something no pharmaceutical blockbuster ever achieved: cutting townwide emergency hospital admissions fourteen percent—by training barbers, cabbies, and Link Workers to weave isolated neighbors back into four hundred local clubs."*
* **Best For:** Public-health summits, hospital boardrooms, and investigative journalism series.

### Option C: "Shoulder to Shoulder" Inside an Australian *Men's Shed* *(Warm, Witty, Authentic)*
* **Visual Hook:** Lathe shavings fly inside a sunlit tin barn in Melbourne; ten retired truckers and electricians banter loudly over tea while building wooden toys for the children's hospital. Hand-painted wooden sign above the door reads: **`MEN DON'T TALK FACE TO FACE — WE TALK SHOULDER TO SHOULDER`**.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Tell a lonely seventy-year-old tradesman to join a 'grief feelings circle,' and he'll stay home watching TV. Ask him to help fix bikes for neighborhood kids on Tuesday morning, and you just saved his life."*
* **Best For:** Short-form features, veterans' & men's health outreach, and community campaigns.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE DIRECTORY

### 1. The "15 Smoldering Cigarettes Extinguished by Choir Sheet Music"
* **Visual Concept:** Stark visual opening where 15 lit cigarettes (representing Holt-Lunstad's mortality-equivalent hazard of isolation) transform seamlessly into 15 wooden organ pipes, garden trowels, and woodworking chisels bursting into warm sound and green shoots.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Macro tabletop metamorphosis shifting from cold ash-grey to sunlit allotment emerald.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Elevates social prescribing instantly from "nice charity" to life-saving clinical cardiology.

### 2. The "Crumpled Pamphlet vs. Open Handshake" (The Warm Handoff)
* **Visual Concept:** Contrasts a lonely senior dropping a medical referral pamphlet into a kitchen bin against a Link Worker knocking gently on Tuesday morning with two travel mugs of tea.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Split-frame doorway shot following Arthur and Nadia side-by-side into the buzzing Salford *Men's Shed*.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Highlights the human craft at the heart of why Link Workers succeed.

### 3. The "*Compassionate Frome* Town Web"
* **Visual Concept:** Overhead night-to-dawn map of a 28,000-person town where barbershops, pharmacies, and taxis pulse with warm golden threads connecting isolated homes before ambulances ever need to roll.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Aerial pull-up juxtaposing Frome's `-14%` emergency hospital curve against neighboring counties' `+28.5%` spike.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves whole towns can vaccinate themselves against loneliness.

### 4. The "Zero White Coats Inside the Shed" Rule
* **Visual Concept:** As Arthur steps across the woodshop threshold, any clinical chart overlay vanishes completely—replaced by a canvas carpenter apron and three eager teenage apprentices asking for his wiring advice.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Warm eye-level medium shot capturing shoulder-to-shoulder dignity.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Reassures viewers that social prescribing empowers community life rather than medicalizing it.
