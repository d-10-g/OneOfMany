# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: PARTICIPATORY MUNICIPAL BUDGETING ([`participatory-municipal-budgeting`](REPORT.md))
## *"Here Is \$28 Million, Neighbors — How Letting 14-Year-Olds and Grandmothers Hold the City Checkbook Transformed 11,000 Cities"*

> **Production Purpose:** This storyboard equips filmmakers, urban-design communicators, and civics educators to bring **Binding Participatory Municipal Budgeting** vividly to life—spanning **Porto Alegre's clean-water miracle**, **Paris's €500M *Cours Oasis* schoolyards**, **Barcelona's open-source *Decidim***, and **teenage budget voters in NYC and Taipei**.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: The "Baking Asphalt to Cooling Forest" Paris Time-Lapse *(Visually Stunning, Joyful)*
* **Visual Hook:** Overhead drone shot of an all-black asphalt Paris elementary schoolyard shimmering at $45^\circ\text{C}$ in summer heat. A giant translucent smartphone ballot labeled `BUDGET PARTICIPATIF DE PARIS — VOTED BY NEIGHBORS & STUDENTS` taps `APPROVE (€420,000)`. Time-lapse jackhammers peel back the tar as shade trees, timber pergolas, and misting fountains burst upward while children and seniors picnic together on Saturday.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"In Paris, ordinary residents—including schoolchildren—don't beg politicians for greener streets. City Hall hands neighbors half a billion euros of the real construction checkbook, and lets them vote directly on what gets built next."*
* **Best For:** Streaming openers, climate-resilient urbanism films, and broad family audiences.

### Option B: Porto Alegre's Pipes & The World Bank Surprise *(Dramatic Historical Proof)*
* **Visual Hook:** 1989 archival textures of muddy, sewerless hillsides in Porto Alegre, Brazil. Pundits on vintage TV scoff: *"Give poor citizens the city budget and they'll waste every cent!"* Cut to World Bank graphs soaring: clean water & sewer connections climbing from `75%` to `98%`, infant mortality plunging, and wealthy residents *voluntarily* paying higher tax compliance because every cent is visible.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Thirty-five years ago, a Brazilian city ran an audacious experiment: let working-class neighborhoods vote directly on up to twenty percent of the capital budget. They didn't buy gimmicks—they built sewers, clinics, and schools, saving thousands of babies' lives."*
* **Best For:** Documentary features, global-development series, and skeptical policy audiences.

### Option C: 15-Year-Old Mateo's \$28 Million Shopping Cart *(Youthful, Kinetic, Interactive)*
* **Visual Hook:** Close-up over 15-year-old Mateo's shoulder at a bustling Queens high-school cafeteria table beside his 71-year-old grandmother. On his screen, a glowing **Knapsack Budget Bar (`$28,000,000 Remaining`)** fills up dynamically as they drag priced engineering cards—`Bioswale Crosswalks ($2.9M)`, `Night Bus Lighting ($1.4M)`—into their real neighborhood cart.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"What happens when you give fourteen-year-olds and their grandmothers twenty-eight million real infrastructure dollars—and force them to balance the engineering math themselves? They become citizens for life."*
* **Best For:** Education summits, youth civic media, and social shorts.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE BUDGET SIMULATOR

### 1. The "Household Grocery Cart for Your Block" (Knapsack Voting)
* **Visual Concept:** Tactile split-screen comparing an old "wish-list suggestion box" (dropped straight into a basement shredder) against a glowing \$28M **Neighborhood Infrastructure Cart** where every tree, bioswale, and solar lamp has a real barcode price and delivery date.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Over-the-shoulder touch interaction as 15-year-old Mateo swaps a flashy fountain for six safe raised crosswalks plus a 10-year maintenance escrow.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Instantly conveys why binding budgets command serious, grown-up civic care.

### 2. The "Glass Pipeline" From Tax Dollar to Park Tree (*Decidim*)
* **Visual Concept:** Instead of tax coins falling into an iron vault, coins roll down a crystal-clear glass tube running right down Main Street, sprouting bus shelters, shaded schoolyards, and safe bike lanes directly in front of the people who paid them.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Continuous street-level tracking shot following one glowing coin from a bakery register to a blooming *Cours Oasis* fig tree.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Rebuilds emotional pride in shared civic investment.

### 3. The "3× Equity Scales" of Paris & Porto Alegre
* **Visual Concept:** Architectural balance beam lifting historically park-starved working-class districts up to sunlit parity via ring-fenced pots and 3× per-capita capital multipliers.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Drone tilt-up from newly paved, clean-water hillsides in Porto Alegre to shaded Paris schoolyards.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows how participatory rules guarantee fairness without pitting neighbor against neighbor.

### 4. The "Civic Training Wheels at Age 14"
* **Visual Concept:** High-school cafeteria ballot booth glowing with real \$200k school-facility dollars, morphing forward four years as the exact same teenagers stride confidently into general-election polling places.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Match-cut on Mateo's hand casting his first age-14 PB ballot and his age-18 municipal ballot.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves democracy is a muscle strengthened by real early practice.
