## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & INTERACTIVE SIMULATOR

### 1. The "Fire Sprinkler vs. Flooding the Whole City" Stabilizer
* **Visual Concept:** Contrasts legacy macro policy (flooding an entire state under 10 feet of water or draining every reservoir to put out one kitchen fire) against ceiling-mounted **Micro-Stabilizer Sprinklers** that mist only the single overheating room within 48 hours.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Architectural cross-section zoom from a 50-state grid down to three Great Lakes manufacturing counties cooling calmly while neighboring homebuilders keep framing houses.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Makes Hall-Woodford heterogeneous stabilization intuitive to any viewer in five seconds.

### 2. The "Encrypted Choir" (Multi-Party Computation)
* **Visual Concept:** Ten singers in soundproof booths hum masked frequencies that sound like static alone, yet combine in the central hall into one crystal-clear chord showing regional economic health—proving nobody has to hand over their diary to know how the town is doing.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Circular 360-degree pan linking separate bank, payroll, and port vaults into a glowing mathematical Laplace shield.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Builds visceral citizen confidence in mathematical privacy guarantees.

### 3. The "Blacked-Out Windshield Dissolving Into Live Radar"
* **Visual Concept:** A bus driver peering nervously into a tiny 90-day-old rearview mirror suddenly watches opaque black paint peel off the front panoramic glass, revealing miles of sunlit highway and early weather markers ahead.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** First-person cockpit POV accelerating smoothly as erratic brake-slams stop completely.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Captures the sheer relief of retiring lagging phone-survey guesswork.

### 4. The "Lock-Gate Canal of Creative Destruction"
* **Visual Concept:** Instead of a dam bursting (mass layoffs) or a stagnant swamp (zombie bailouts), precision canal locks gently lift skilled machinists from legacy lines straight onto higher-paying clean-tech vessels.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Overhead fluid simulation illustrating Mokyr, Aghion, and Howitt's 2025 Nobel growth mechanics.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows how dynamism and worker security reinforce each other.
