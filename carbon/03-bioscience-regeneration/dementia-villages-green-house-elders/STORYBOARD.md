# STORYBOARD & CREATIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE: DEMENTIA VILLAGES & GREEN HOUSE COTTAGES ([`dementia-villages-green-house-elders`](./REPORT.md))
## *"No More Locked Fluorescent Hallways — How Dutch Dementia Villages, 10-Elder Medicaid Hearths & Gentle Lift Robotics Bring Aging Home"*

> **Production Purpose:** This empathetic 3-act documentary and civic design storyboard dismantles the cruel myth that Alzheimer's or frailty requires 120-bed institutional nursing corridors—showing how **De Hogeweyk** in Weesp (funded on the exact same Dutch statutory *Wlz* daily budget as traditional nursing wards), France's **Village Landais Alzheimer**, and **~400 U.S. Green House cottages** (~45% Medicaid) cut hospital readmissions **16%–30%** and pandemic mortality **4×–5×**, amplified by **camera-free radar** and **gentle tactile lift robotics**.

---

## PART 1: THREE ALTERNATIVE NARRATIVE OPENINGS (DIRECTOR'S OPTIONS)

### Option A: "The 4-Digit Keypad Door vs. The Birch-Shaded Bakery Square" *(Visceral Split-Screen Contrast)*
* **Visual Hook:** Split-screen at 4:00 PM ("sundowning" hour). Left (`120-Bed Institutional Wing`): A retired choir teacher in hospital-slipper socks tugs anxiously at a disguised grey steel exit door with a locked 4-digit keypad under buzzing fluorescent tubes while a sedative syringe is prepped at the nurses' station. Right (`De Hogeweyk, Weesp`): **Willem Bakker** buttons his wool coat, opens a normal wooden front door onto a sunny brick Dutch square, picks out Boskoop apples at the village grocer, and strolls beneath birch trees to choir practice.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"When an eighty-year-old with Alzheimer's pulls on a locked hospital hallway door at four PM, medicine calls it 'agitated exit-seeking' and reaches for sedatives. In Weesp, Netherlands, they asked a simpler question: what if we unlocked the front door into a real, safe neighborhood—on the exact same public nursing budget?"*
* **Best For:** Mainstream families, public broadcasters, and Alzheimer's caregiver audiences.

### Option B: "The \$20,000 Resort Myth — Opened on Standard Medicaid & Dutch Public Tariffs" *(Fiscal Reveal)*
* **Visual Hook:** Camera glides past fresh garlic cornbread baking in a sunlit **Tupelo, Mississippi Green House** kitchen and café tables in **Weesp** and **Dax, France**, while an on-screen price ticker drops from a crossed-out **\$20,000/mo Boutique Myth** down to `100% Standard Dutch Wlz Tariff & ~45% U.S. Medicaid`.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Everyone who sees De Hogeweyk or a ten-elder Green House cottage assumes it's a resort for millionaires. In reality, nearly half of U.S. Green House cottages serve Medicaid elders—and cut hospital readmissions up to thirty percent."*
* **Best For:** State Medicaid directors, governors, legislative budget committees, and hospital boards.

### Option C: "Two Kinds of Elder Robotics: Cold Plastic Companions vs. Gentle Human Lift Cradles" *(Humane Tech Pivot)*
* **Visual Hook:** Left: A lonely senior staring blankly at a talking plastic tablet screen in a sterile room. Right: 62-year-old certified *shahbaz* **Clara Robinson** laughs warmly with 86-year-old **Maeve Washington** while a whisper-quiet, quilted **soft-tactile transfer cradle** lifts Maeve effortlessly from bed to chair with zero strain on Clara's spine, beneath a **lens-free 60-GHz ceiling radar tile**.
* **Voiceover Core:** *"Robots should never replace the human hand holding yours at eighty-five. Robots belong under the heavy lift—ending caregiver back injuries forever so warm human beings can stay at the hearth table."*
* **Best For:** Gerontechnology summits, healthcare labor unions, and design-ethics forums.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHOR BANK & CINEMATOGRAPHY CUES

### 1. "The Vanishing Nurses' Station Into the Hearth Table"
* **Visual Concept:** Tabletop architectural match-cut: a sterile laminate hospital nurses' counter stacked with plastic binders dissolves seamlessly into a warm 12-seat oak family dining table (*convivium*) steamed by fresh vegetable soup.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Slow 35mm dolly push-in shifting from harsh overhead 6,500 K fluorescent glare to warm natural garden sidelight.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Shows in three seconds how clinical observation becomes deeper and kinder when shared over dinner rather than behind plexiglass.

### 2. "Lens-Free Radar Ripples vs. The Intrusive Camera Eye"
* **Visual Concept:** An intrusive black security camera lens shatters into harmless golden **60-GHz millimeter-wave ripples** in the ceiling corner, rendering only an anonymous geometric balance vector as an elder steps safely toward the bathroom.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Clean volumetric point-cloud overlay demonstrating zero optical recording.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Proves night safety never requires sacrificing an elder's bedroom or bathroom modesty.

### 3. "Quilted Hands Under the Heavy Lift"
* **Visual Concept:** Biomechanical overlay showing red lumbar compression arrows vanishing from 62-year-old *shahbaz* Clara's spine into quilted elastomeric transfer arms while her own hands remain completely free to hold Maeve's hand.
* **Camera / Animation Cue:** Gentle two-shot eye-level framing keeping human warmth in the foreground and robotics quietly underneath.
* **Emotional Takeaway:** Illustrates the Carbon bright line: machines carry the weight; humans carry the relationship.
