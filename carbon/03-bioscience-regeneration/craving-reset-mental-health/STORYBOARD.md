# Silencing the Hijacked Brain — Visual Storyboard & Documentary Guide
**Pillar 3: Bioscience, Medicine & Planetary Regeneration ([`craving-reset-mental-health`](REPORT.md)) — Carbon Edition**

> **Production Purpose:** This 4-minute documentary replaces fatalism and stigma around fentanyl, meth, depression, and schizophrenia with fierce, evidence-backed hope. Grounded in 2024–2026 clinical breakthroughs—**GLP-1/GIP craving suppression**, **WVU Dr. Ali Rezai's 30-minute Focused Ultrasound (LIFU) helmet**, **Stanford's 5-day SAINT depression remission**, **Cobenfy non-dopamine schizophrenia care**, and **Trieste/Portugal community recovery co-ops**—it shows how humanity heals the hijacked brain in days rather than punishing it for decades.

## Part 1: Three Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "The Radio Static Just Switched Off" (The 30-Minute WVU Helmet Hook)
* **Visual Hook:** Open inside West Virginia University's Rockefeller Neuroscience Institute: 34-year-old veteran Marcus sits wide awake wearing an incisionless acoustic ultrasound helmet. As fentanyl trigger images flash on a monitor—cues that spiked his heart rate to 135 BPM four days earlier—we watch his live pulse hold rock-steady at 64 BPM while tears of quiet relief fill his eyes.
* **Core Conflict:** Shatters the cruel 70-year myth that addiction is a moral defect curable by lectures, handcuffs, or 85%-relapse abstinence camps.
* **Emotional Payoff:** Deep, visceral catharsis watching a human being get their own mind back in thirty minutes without a single surgical cut.
* **Best For:** General audiences, families touched by the opioid/meth crisis, veterans, and local civic leaders.

### Angle B: "Five Days Out of the Dark" (The Restorative Psychiatry Hook)
* **Visual Hook:** Follow 27-year-old cellist Sofia through five sunlit mornings of Stanford **SAINT** fMRI-guided magnetic neuromodulation—intercut with her brother Leo taking *Cobenfy*, the first non-dopamine schizophrenia capsule in 70 years, keeping his creativity and warmth intact without zombie sedation.
* **Core Conflict:** Confronts the despair of 3-month psychiatry waitlists, trial-and-error SSRIs, and locked 20th-century psychiatric wards.
* **Emotional Payoff:** Joyous musical crescendo as Sofia picks up her cello bow on Saturday morning inside an unlocked Trieste-style café cooperative.
* **Best For:** Young adults, mental-health advocates, clinicians, and families navigating severe depression or psychosis.

### Angle C: "Biology + Rat Park: Why Morgantown Met Trieste" (The Systems Solution Hook)
* **Visual Hook:** Contrast two street-level realities: a chaotic 2024 ER hallway / county jail booking desk versus a warm, unlocked 24/7 Neuro-Sanctuary that connects biological craving resets directly into salaried timber-framing and culinary social cooperatives modeled on Trieste and Portugal.
* **Core Conflict:** Answers why *both* unmedicated "housing-only" permissiveness and punitive incarceration failed—and why pairing subcortical biology with purposeful squad work cuts relapse below 20%.
* **Emotional Payoff:** Civic pride watching former overdose survivors restore historic wooden bridges in their hometown.
* **Best For:** Mayors, public-health officials, policy skeptics, and urban-renewal audiences.

## Part 3: Visual Metaphors, Cinematography, Sound & Verified Citations

### Visual Metaphor Bank
* **The Screaming Smoke Alarm vs. Acoustic Tuning Fork:**
  * *Visual Execution:* Portrays fentanyl craving as a malfunctioning hardwired smoke detector blaring at 110 decibels inside the skull—and LIFU + GLP-1 therapy as resetting the sensor cleanly without ripping out the house wiring.
  * *Audience Takeaway:* Silencing craving isn't erasing free will; it is turning off a false suffocation alarm so free will works again.
* **The Open Garden Gate of San Giovanni (Trieste):**
  * *Visual Execution:* Real footage of Trieste's former psychiatric hospital wall, demolished and turned into a rose garden and lively worker-run restaurant (`Il Posto delle Fragole`).
  * *Audience Takeaway:* Open doors paired with real medicine and paid work are safer and kinder than locked wards.
* **The Eight-Person Timber Beam Lift:**
  * *Visual Execution:* Tactile physical metaphor showing why recovery sticks when seven teammates literally depend on your shoulder at 8:00 AM Monday.
  * *Audience Takeaway:* Biology quiets the craving; belonging cures the isolation.

### Production Ethics & Anti-Stigma Guidelines
* **Dignity First—Zero "Misery Porn" B-Roll:** Never film identifiable vulnerable people in street distress or use lurid needle close-ups. Center every frame on human agency, peer solidarity, crisp neurobiology, and skilled craftsmanship.
* **Clear Safety Framing on Neuromodulation:** Visually distinguish gentle, non-thermal, incisionless **Low-Intensity** Focused Ultrasound (`LIFU`) and magnetic **SAINT TMS** from historical electroshock or surgical ablation so viewers feel immediate calm confidence.
* **Family Voices:** Include a 15-second beat with a mother and her recovered son cooking Sunday dinner together to anchor the emotional stakes.

### Cinematography, Lighting & Sound Architecture
* **Acoustic Storytelling (Crucial):** Open Scene 1 with an oppressive, high-pitched sub-bass tinnitus hum layered under chaotic street sirens—letting the audience *feel* subcortical craving noise. At the exact midpoint of Scene 3's LIFU ultrasound session, cut the hum to **complete, breathtaking studio silence**, followed only by Marcus's calm breathing and soft acoustic cello.
* **Color & Lens Language:** Shoot Morgantown and Trieste on warm, organic 35mm anamorphic glass—amber wood workshops, steam rising off espresso cups, crisp winter sunlight—avoiding every sensationalized green-tinted "drug documentary" cliché.
