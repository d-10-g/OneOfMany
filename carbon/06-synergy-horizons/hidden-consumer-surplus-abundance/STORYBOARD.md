# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: THE INVISIBLE \$35,000 RAISE ([`hidden-consumer-surplus-abundance`](REPORT.md))
## *"Seeing the Miracles Already on Our Kitchen Table — Without Ever Gaslighting Families About Rent — and Unfreezing What Still Hurts"*

> **Production Purpose:** This deeply empathetic 4:30 flagship film cures both cynical doom ("nothing ever gets better") and tone-deaf tech-bro gaslighting ("stop complaining about rent, you have an iPhone!"). Viewers step into **Marisol Vega's Queens kitchen** across 1998, 2026, and 2033—seeing the **+\$35,000/year in verified consumer surplus** (**Brynjolfsson *PNAS* GDP-B** & **Chatterji NBER WP 34255**) *already* transforming family life, then watching clean energy, robotics, and humane civic rules pull housing, medicine, and care onto that exact same abundance curve.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "The \$20 Calling Card vs. Bedtime With Abuela" *(Deeply Emotional, Immigrant Family Heart)*
* **Hook:** Warm 16mm flashback to **1998 Jackson Heights, Queens**: Marisol's mother clutching a **\$20 bodega calling card**, watching a wall clock tick nervously through twelve scratchy audio minutes with Puebla, Mexico. Seamless match-cut to **2026**: the exact same oak table, where **Abuela Lucía** laughs in crystal-clear HD video for forty-five minutes every night reading stories to her grandchildren for **\$0.00**—right beside Marisol's very real **\$2,450 rent envelope**.
* **Visual Arc:** Validates working-class budget stress with total respect while restoring wonder at the miracles humanity already built—then unfreezes the rent envelope in Act III.
* **Best For:** Broad general audiences, working families, and flagship homepage opening.

### Angle B: "The \$50,000 Living Room From 1994 Inside One Jacket Pocket" *(Playful Tabletop Unpack)*
* **Hook:** A warehouse stage packed with **>\$50,000 of 1994 gear**: a 32-volume *Britannica*, VHS camcorder, Garmin GPS, darkroom, international teleconferencing rig, \$3,000 hospital pulse-oximeter cart, and language-tutor desk—all shrinking playfully into Marisol's coat pocket while an official 1934 **GDP Meter** stubbornly reads **\$0.00 VALUE ADDED**.
* **Visual Arc:** Eye-opening economic visual essay featuring **Prof. Erik Brynjolfsson** (*PNAS* GDP-B) and **Dr. Ronnie Chatterji** (NBER WP 34255).
* **Best For:** Economic explainers, educators, journalists, and tech-skeptic viewers.

### Angle C: "The Wishbone Chart: Why Half of Life Got 99% Cheaper While Three Things Tripled" *(Diagnostic Clarity)*
* **Hook:** Giant glowing floor projection of the **BLS Two-Speed Economy Chart (2000–2026)**: blue lines plunge downward (-99.8% solar, -97% batteries, -99.9% genomes & calls) while three red lines shoot through the ceiling (Housing, Hospital Billing, Childcare). The host walks onto the chart and physically unlocks the three 1970s padlocks holding the red lines up.
* **Visual Arc:** Turns economic confusion into an empowering civic action plan.
* **Best For:** Policy summits, mayors, healthcare reformers, and classroom curricula.

## 3. Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Motif Bank

* **1. The "1994 \$50,000 Electronics Cart Shrinking Into One Pocket":**
  * *Visual:* Playful Wes Anderson-style overhead shot of 18 bulky 1990s machines (encyclopedia shelves, camcorder, GPS, dictaphone, atlas, medical textbook, calling-card stack) folding like origami into a modest \$200 phone.
  * *Audio Cue:* Mechanical clicks, cassette whirs, and paper rustles resolving into one warm chime.
* **2. The "1934 Blindfolded Accountant Stamp":**
  * *Visual:* Brass vintage rubber stamp hitting a joyful family video call with **GDP VALUE: \$0.00** while stamping an ambulance billing error **GDP VALUE: +\$3,800**.
  * *Audio Cue:* Heavy bureaucratic iron thud contrasted against children's laughter from Puebla.
* **3. Three Rusty 1970s Padlocks Opening Into Wood, Sunlight & Greenhouses:**
  * *Visual:* Tactile iron padlocks labeled `PARKING/ZONING FREEZE`, `35% BILLING CODERS`, and `PEAK FOSSIL GRID` clicking open to reveal Cross-Laminated Timber grain, a friendly neighborhood nurse stethoscope, and solar-lit strawberries.
  * *Audio Cue:* Three crisp brass lock-releases opening into a soaring string chord.
* **4. The Wishbone Curve Reunion:**
  * *Visual:* Glowing red cost lines for Shelter, Healthcare, and Care bending smoothly downward across an architectural floor grid to braid with the deep-blue solar and genome curves.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `Same Physics. Unfrozen Rules. Shared Abundance.`

## 4. Cinematography, Sound Design & Production Bible

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **1998 Flashback:** Warm tungsten bodega neon and soft 16mm photochemical grain.
* **2026 Split Reality:** Cozy tablet glow contrasted against cool fluorescent bill-paying stress—opening in **2033** into sunlit mass-timber honey tones and lush Queens balcony greenery on wide 35mm lenses.

* **Opening:** Bodega payphone coin clatter and busy-signal pulse dissolving into Abuela's warm voice.
