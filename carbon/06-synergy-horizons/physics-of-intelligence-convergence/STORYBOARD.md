## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "The 20-Watt Brain vs. The 120,000-Watt Rack" (Vacuum Tubes Reborn as Physics)
* **Hook:** Places a dim **20-watt hallway lightbulb** beside a human head—and a roaring **120,000-watt industrial server rack** next to it—asking *why* 2024 AI burned 6,000× more energy than biology! Reveals that extrapolating 2024 copper GPUs was just like 1955 pundits extrapolating hot vacuum tubes, before **thermodynamic p-bits, laser photonics, and altermagnets** dropped probabilistic sampling energy **1,000× to 10,000×**.
* **Visual Arc:** Glowing red 1950s vacuum tubes & hot copper wires $\rightarrow$ Cool violet laser waveguides & room-temperature electron dance $\rightarrow$ Quiet solar-powered clinic box.
* **Best For:** Mainstage keynotes, climate-anxious tech skeptics, and broad science audiences.

### Angle B: "Surfing Thermal Noise Instead of Fighting It" (Extropic Z1 + Hilbert's 6th Rhyme)
* **Hook:** Zooms inside a room-temperature transistor where electrons naturally jiggle ($k_B T$): old digital chips burned megawatts building walls to *crush* that jiggle, then burned more megawatts *faking* randomness! **Extropic's Z1** simply opens the gate and lets nature's free thermal dance sample AI probabilities directly—rhyming poetically with **Hilbert's 6th Problem** deriving smooth fluid laws from random atomic collisions!
* **Visual Arc:** Rigid digital soldiers marching in exhausting lockstep vs. effortless ocean surfers riding natural thermal waves into clean Boltzmann distributions.
* **Best For:** Physicists, hardware engineers, students, and deep-tech enthusiasts.

### Angle C: "Solving Amara's 98% 'Dark Genome' Mystery on a Toaster's Wattage" (*AlphaGenome 2*)
* **Hook:** Follows 6-year-old Amara into a solar-powered clinic: standard gene tests missed her rare disease because 98% of DNA is non-coding. On a 400-watt photonic-thermodynamic desktop, **DeepMind's *AlphaGenome 2*** reads **1 million DNA letters** conditioned on *one exact gut immune cell state*, spots the hidden single-letter switch, and designs a Lean/physics-verified CRISPR cure in 18 minutes!
* **Visual Arc:** Mysterious dark 98% DNA spiral lighting up in one exact macrophage cell type $\rightarrow$ Instant thermodynamic molecular lock $\rightarrow$ Healthy child laughing outdoors.
* **Best For:** Healthcare leaders, families, biotech summits, and Global South clinic showcases.
