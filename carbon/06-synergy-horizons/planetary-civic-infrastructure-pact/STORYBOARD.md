# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: THE GREAT INFRASTRUCTURE PEACE
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`planetary-civic-infrastructure-pact`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** Urgent civic-healing film (`isUrgentSocial: true`, runtime: 4:20) showing mayors, farmers, ratepayers, and clean-tech builders how to end bitter NIMBY wars overnight—turning water-guzzling server boxes and sterile gravel solar lots into **65°C home-heating conservatories, +30%-yield shaded berry farms, repowered coal switchyards, and dividend-paying town co-ops**.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "January Strawberries Heated by AI" (The Duluth / Mäntsälä Reveal)
* **Hook:** Opens in a `-18°C` blizzard—then steps inside a lush, humid public glass conservatory bursting with ripe strawberries and lemon trees, revealing that **100% of the warmth comes free from closed-loop $65^\circ\text{C}$ water cooling a zero-evaporation AI compute hall next door** while cutting 18,000 neighbors' heating bills in half!
* **Visual Arc:** Freezing winter breath & angry town-hall signs $\rightarrow$ Warm copper heat-exchange pipes $\rightarrow$ Families in sweaters picking winter fruit as utility bills drop.
* **Best For:** Mainstage keynotes, municipal town halls, and general family viewers.

### Angle B: "More Berries AND Clean Power on the Same Acre" (The Farmer's Smile)
* **Hook:** Follows a skeptical fourth-generation farmer walking beneath $3.5\text{-meter}$-high **agrivoltaic solar trellises**: instead of scorching in $41^\circ\text{C}$ July sun, her tomatoes and grazing sheep thrive in dappled shade with **35% less irrigation water**—doubling farm revenue without losing an inch of soil!
* **Visual Arc:** Sunburned, cracked dirt vs. lush emerald pasture under elevated tracking glass panels.
* **Best For:** Rural counties, agricultural associations, and land-use policy briefings.

### Angle C: "From NIMBY Lawsuit to December Dividend Check" (The 5-Clause Pact)
* **Hook:** Contrasts a 4-year courtroom gridlock over an extractive fenced facility with a town that passes the **5-Clause Symbiotic Infrastructure Charter**—plugging into a retired coal switchyard, protecting residential power bills, and mailing every local household a **\$420 December energy co-op dividend**.
* **Visual Arc:** Tense gavel-banging stalemate dissolving into an **89% green Polis consensus vote** and a celebratory ribbon-cutting in 48 days.
* **Best For:** Governors, county commissioners, utility regulators, and infrastructure investors.
