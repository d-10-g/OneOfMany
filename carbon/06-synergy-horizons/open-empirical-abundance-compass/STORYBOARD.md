## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "Why Your Free Sunday Afternoon Shows Up as \$0.00 in GDP" (The 1934 Speedometer Flaw)
* **Hook:** Opens in a 1934 steel foundry where Simon Kuznets invented GDP to count iron ingots—then cuts to 2026 where free Wikipedia, free WhatsApp calls to Grandma, and free AI homework/health guidance (**70%+ of real usage in Chatterji's NBER study!**) register as **\$0 in GDP**! Unveils the modern **Abundance Compass** that counts real human time and thriving.
* **Visual Arc:** Sepia 1934 smokestack clipboard $\rightarrow$ Blind \$0.00 cash register $\rightarrow$ Radiant live civic dashboard measuring hours saved, median cash, and Global South inclusion.
* **Best For:** Mainstream audiences, economics explainers, and policy keynotes.

### Angle B: "Zero Human Eyes on Your Chat—Crystal-Clear Eyes on the Economy" (Cleanroom Magic)
* **Hook:** Tackles privacy skepticism head-on: follows a nurse's late-night chat and a small shop's payroll passing through a **cryptographic Differential-Privacy Clean Room** where *zero humans ever read personal rows*, yet an instant 7-day regional map alerts town leaders to fund **90%-wage retraining guilds** months before layoffs sting!
* **Visual Arc:** Frosted cryptographic glass locking individual names tight while illuminating crisp, warm neighborhood opportunity maps—paired with **Hall & Woodford's 2026 Nobel** stabilization insight.
* **Best For:** Privacy advocates, central bankers, labor unions, and tech ethicists.

### Angle C: "A Baker, a Nurse, and a Welder at the Helm" (Sortition + Polis in Action)
* **Hook:** Follows 75 randomly chosen everyday citizens (like jury duty, paid and respected!) walking into Ostbelgien / Guadalajara's **Citizens' Assembly**, reading the live Cleanroom Abundance Dashboard, checking an **86%-consensus *Polis* map** from 14,000 neighbors, and steering local AI dividends into schools and care guilds in one weekend!
* **Visual Arc:** Cable-news shouting heads switching `OFF` $\rightarrow$ Sunlit circular wooden assembly table where real neighbors listen, check live data, and agree.
* **Best For:** Civic reformers, fatigued voters, and democracy summits.
