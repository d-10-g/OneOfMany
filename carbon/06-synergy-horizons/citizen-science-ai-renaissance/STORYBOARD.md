# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: THE AMATEUR GOLDEN AGE
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`citizen-science-ai-renaissance`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** A deeply energizing cross-pillar synthesis film (runtime: 4:15) proving that AI and open satellites didn't make ordinary humans obsolete—they shattered 20th-century credential gatekeeping! Unites **Pietro Miotti's 48-agent Maya LiDAR sweep**, the **Vesuvius Challenge** students reading charred Roman scrolls, **Vera C. Rubin Zooniverse** comet hunters, and **high-school creek eDNA blitzes**.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "48 Agents & One Curious Weekend" (The Pietro Miotti LiDAR Hook)
* **Hook:** Opens on a traveler with a laptop in Peru asking *"What would Indiana Jones build in 2026?"*—spinning up **48 parallel AI agents** to scan $1,200\text{ km}^2$ of public NASA jungle laser maps in under 72 hours, uncovering an oval Maya pyramid missing from government registries, and teaming up respectfully with academic Mayanists!
* **Visual Arc:** Lush opaque green jungle canopy peeling away into crisp 3D LiDAR elevation stone $\rightarrow$ 48 tiled agent windows filtering terrain $\rightarrow$ Joyous citizen-scholar handshake.
* **Best For:** Tech-curious general audiences, builders, and viral documentary openers.

### Angle B: "Reading Charcoal After 2,000 Years" (Vesuvius + Rubin + eDNA Montage)
* **Hook:** Intercuts three young "outsiders" rewriting textbooks in the same week: a college student seeing ancient **Greek letters glow inside a lump of Herculaneum volcanic charcoal**, a Chilean teenager flagging an active comet 60 seconds after **Vera Rubin's** shutter clicks, and an Oaxaca biology class finding a "lost" salamander in a \$40 creek water filter.
* **Visual Arc:** Rapid, goosebump-inducing cross-cuts across space, antiquity, and living nature—united by zero-paywall open science.
* **Best For:** Youth STEM inspiration, classrooms, museums, and anti-doomerism reels.

### Angle C: "Reclaiming the True Meaning of 'Amateur'" (Darwin to Zooniverse)
* **Hook:** Reminds viewers that *amateur* comes from *amator*—doing science for love—connecting Mary Anning and backyard radio pioneer Grote Reber directly to 2.7 million Zooniverse citizens and retiree-student guilds solving protein folding (*Eterna*) and cosmic mysteries together.
* **Visual Arc:** Warm 19th-century brass microscope morphing into a glowing public-library Chromebook linked to Cerro Pachón and synchrotron X-ray beams.
* **Best For:** Lifelong-learning communities, libraries, and post-labor meaning discussions.
