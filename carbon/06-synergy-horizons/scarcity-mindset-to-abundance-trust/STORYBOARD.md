# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: HEALING THE ZERO-SUM BRAIN ([`scarcity-mindset-to-abundance-trust`](REPORT.md))
## *"Why Human Beings Aren't Naturally Mean — They're Just Carrying a 13-IQ-Point Scarcity Tax, and How Free Energy + Fair Ownership Gives Us Our Grace Back"*

> **Production Purpose:** This 4:30 philosophical and scientific centerpiece of **OneOfMany** reveals *why* technical abundance (essentially free clean energy + physical/knowledge automation) and humane social conventions are two halves of one key. Grounded in **Mullainathan & Shafir's 2013 *Science* cognitive-bandwidth discovery (+13–14 IQ points)** and **Benjamin M. Friedman's 200-year moral history**, the film follows Tamil Nadu farmers and Youngstown machinist **Derrick Hollis** from survival tunneling to high-trust civic generosity.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "Same Brain, Six Weeks Later" (The 2013 *Science* Harvest Experiment)
* **Hook:** Open in sunlit **Tamil Nadu, India**: a warm, hardworking sugarcane farmer takes a visual pattern puzzle two weeks *before* harvest while worried about overdue bills—missing half the answers. Cut six weeks forward to the **exact same man** right *after* harvest payments clear his debts: calm, smiling, solving complex puzzles effortlessly—scoring **13 to 14 IQ points higher**!
* **Visual Arc:** Shatters the cruel myth that stressed citizens are "irrational" or "tribal," connecting brain bandwidth directly to Youngstown, Ohio, and the Carbon Synergy Engine.
* **Best For:** Flagship documentary opening, behavioral-science fans, and cross-partisan empathy building.

### Angle B: "Why Half a Key Never Opens the Door" (Tech Alone vs. Rules Alone)
* **Hook:** Tactile brass lock labeled `HUMAN HIGH-TRUST PROSPERITY`. First, a glowing blue half-key (`ROBOTS + FUSION ALONE`) turns halfway and jams against worker layoff panic. Next, a glowing amber half-key (`REDISTRIBUTION WITHOUT GROWTH`) jams against zero-sum rationing anger. Then both halves click together into **one complete key**—and the heavy iron door swings wide into sunlit abundance.
* **Visual Arc:** Unforgettable 20-second metaphor explaining the entire *OneOfMany* architecture.
* **Best For:** Homepage manifesto reel, policy leaders, and tech/humanities bridge audiences.

### Angle C: "Derrick's Town Hall: 2024 Shouting Match vs. 2033 Welcome Dinner"
* **Hook:** Contrast an exhausted **Derrick Hollis** in a tense 2024 Youngstown public hearing—terrified of plant closures and utility hikes—with **May 2033**: Derrick, backed by a **90% Guild Bridge**, **\$14/mo solar-fusion power**, and a **\$900/mo Citizen Compute Dividend**, coaching teen robotics and hosting a backyard potluck welcoming thirty new technician families to town.
* **Visual Arc:** Deeply moving American heartland story of restored pride, patience, and neighborliness.
* **Best For:** Working-class communities, union halls, civic groups, and festival screenings.
