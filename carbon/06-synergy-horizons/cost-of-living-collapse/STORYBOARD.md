# CARBON EDITION STORYBOARD: THE HOUSEHOLD ABUNDANCE DIVIDEND
## Visual Narrative & Creative Production Guide ([`cost-of-living-collapse`](REPORT.md))

> **Production Purpose:** This flagship synthesis storyboard brings the entire **Carbon Edition** home to a single oak kitchen table in a thrilling 4:30 visual film. Viewers watch a median four-person family's suffocating **\$7,400/month 2026 budget** drop line-by-line to **\$2,150/month by 2035 (-71%)** as sub-penny clean energy, factory mass-timber homes, autonomous electric pods, precision biology, and 1.5% Homestead rules converge simultaneously.

---

## 1. Three Alternative Narrative Angles (Directors' Options)

### Angle A: "Six Envelopes on the Kitchen Table" (The \$7,400 $\rightarrow$ \$2,150 Countdown)
* **Hook:** Overhead shot of Chidi and Nadia's kitchen table in 2026: six heavy stacks of monthly bills totaling **\$7,400/month**—Housing (\$2,600), Childcare/Tuition (\$1,200), Health Insurance (\$1,100), Groceries (\$950), Two Cars (\$850), Utilities/Debt (\$700). Then pull each stack one-by-one through the **Carbon Synergy Engine**, watching the counter spin down to **\$1,374 core essentials (\$2,150 with joy & travel!)** while quality of life skyrockets.
* **Visual Arc:** Satisfying, tactile tabletop financial countdown paired with vivid glimpses into all six Carbon Atlas pillars.
* **Best For:** Flagship homepage explainer, viral family-finance sharing, and broad general audiences.

### Angle B: "Why TVs Got 99% Cheaper While Rent Tripled—And How Everything Joins the TV Curve"
* **Hook:** Open in an electronics aisle: a wall-sized 4K screen costs \$250 while a 15-minute ambulance ride or one month's rent costs \$2,600. Explain *Baumol's Cost Disease* visually—then quote **Dr. Tony Seba** showing what happens when housing, energy, food, transport, and medicine all jump onto **clean-tech & robotic learning curves at once**.
* **Visual Arc:** Crisp economic animation weaving into factory mass-timber cranes, vertical strawberry greenhouses, and solar roofs.
* **Best For:** Economists, tech skeptics, business viewers, and policy thinkers.

### Angle C: "Friday Afternoon Off Together" (The Okafor Family's 22-Hour Week)
* **Hook:** Contrast Chidi stuck in 75-minute Atlanta bumper-to-bumper traffic in 2026 with **April 2035**: Chidi restoring a sunlit creek in the morning, Nadia conducting a neighborhood choir, their third baby laughing on the porch, and **both parents free every Friday afternoon** because thriving no longer requires ninety hours of survival grind.
* **Visual Arc:** Intimate emotional story showing that the real dividend of cheap physical abundance is **restored family time and love**.
* **Best For:** Working parents, human-interest storytelling, and festival documentary openings.

## 3. Visual Metaphor & B-Roll Motif Bank

* **Six Red Bill Stacks Shrinking Into One Green Seedling Ledger:**
  * *Visual:* Stop-motion tabletop choreography where six towering stacks of 2026 bills shrink down 71% on an oak table, making room for a bowl of fresh local strawberries, a violin, and baby shoes.
  * *Audio Cue:* Crisp, descending marimba cascade as each monthly number drops (`$7,400` $\rightarrow$ `$4,850` $\rightarrow$ `$3,100` $\rightarrow$ `$2,150`), ending in a warm breath of relief.
* **The Parked SUV Turning Into a Music Studio:**
  * *Visual:* Seamless wipe inside a suburban garage where a dusty \$650/month second car dissolves into birch acoustic panels, upright piano, and sunny garden doors.
* **The Six-Gear Synergy Clockwork:**
  * *Visual:* Six colored hexagonal gears matching the Carbon Edition's 6 Pillars interlocking so one turn of the Amber Energy gear spins Robotics, Bio, Society, and Space into household abundance.
  * *Audio Cue:* Precision wooden-and-brass clockwork clicking effortlessly into harmonic resonance.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `Energy × Robotics × Bio × Fair Norms = 71% Cost Collapse`.
* **Strawberries Picked Two Miles Away at Dawn:**
  * *Visual:* Dew-covered organic strawberries lifted gently by soft robotic grippers under sub-penny solar LEDs at 6:00 AM, arriving on Nadia's breakfast table at 7:30 AM—zero 1,500-mile diesel semi-trucks.
  * *On-Screen Label:* `1,500 Diesel Miles` $\rightarrow$ `2 Solar Miles`.
* **Friday 1:00 PM Closed Laptop:**
  * *Visual:* Chidi and Nadia closing their guild tablets on Friday at 1:00 PM without a flicker of bill anxiety, stepping out onto the porch with their three children.

## 4. Cinematography, Sound Design & Cross-Pillar Production Bible

### Color Palette & Lighting Progression
* **Act I (2026 Squeeze):** Tight late-night overhead pendant light, clutter of paper statements, and rush-hour brake-light red glare through wet windshields.
* **Act II & III (2035 Convergence):** Sun-drenched morning honey timber, emerald vertical-farm greens, turquoise clean-water shimmer, and golden Friday-afternoon porch warmth on wide 35mm lenses.
