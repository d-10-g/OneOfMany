# STORYBOARD & VISUAL PRODUCTION GUIDE — CARBON EDITION
## Pathway 5.11: Filming the Cosmic Time-Lapse — Vera C. Rubin LSST, Dwarf Planet 2026 ED138 & Citizen Sky Patrols

> **Production Intent:**
> Capture the awe and democratic joy of **June 30, 2026**, when humanity stopped taking static space snapshots and switched on a 3,200-megapixel, 10-year cosmic movie camera on Cerro Pachón—linking the discovery of distant dwarf planet candidate **2026 ED138** directly to teenagers hunting real comets from home on **Zooniverse**.

## PART 1: THREE NARRATIVE ANGLES & DIRECTOR HOOKS

### Angle A: "The Greatest Movie Ever Filmed" (June 30, 2026 Shutter Open)
* **Core Hook:** Contrasts a tiny keyhole view through Hubble/JWST with the roaring mechanical grace of **Vera C. Rubin Observatory's** 8.4-meter Simonyi Telescope snapping **45 full Moons per exposure** every 35 seconds—revealing ten million pulsing, moving cosmic sparks per night!
* **Emotional Arc:** Quiet reverence under Chilean desert stars $\rightarrow$ Kinetic marvel of SLAC's car-sized 3.2-gigapixel lens $\rightarrow$ Exhilaration as a static sky comes alive in 4D motion.
* **Visual Signature:** Zoom-out from a single 4K screen to a glowing stadium wall of **378 ultra-HD monitors** needed to show just *one* Rubin frame at 1:1 resolution.
* **Best For:** Planetarium domes, flagship keynote openers, and broad family audiences.

### Angle B: "A New World Beyond Pluto: 2026 ED138" (Outer Solar System Hunt)
* **Core Hook:** Follows astrophysicists Matthew Holman and Kevin Napier linking faint moving dots across early Rubin frames—and tracing precovery ghosts back to 1937 plates—to unveil **2026 ED138**, a 600–1,500 km icy dwarf planet candidate gliding silently in the deep dark beyond Neptune.
* **Emotional Arc:** Mystery of unexplored outer darkness $\rightarrow$ Detective thrill of algorithmic tracklet linking $\rightarrow$ Wonder that our own solar system still hides giant secret worlds.
* **Visual Signature:** Three faint gold pinpricks stepping cleanly across a sea of fixed background galaxies while a sunlit ice-world render emerges from shadow.
* **Best For:** Space exploration enthusiasts, science channels, and classroom astronomy units.

### Angle C: "Homework That Discovers a Comet" (Zooniverse Citizen Sky Patrol)
* **Core Hook:** Follows 16-year-old Camila in Valparaíso opening **Rubin Comet Catchers** and **Galaxy Zoo: Rubin** 60 seconds after shutter close—flagging a real sublimating active asteroid alongside volunteers in Kenya and Ohio.
* **Emotional Arc:** Teenage screen boredom $\rightarrow$ Shock that live mountaintop data isn't paywalled $\rightarrow$ Pure pride seeing student names on official discoveries.
* **Visual Signature:** Split-screen tracing a photon from an icy comet tail $\rightarrow$ Cerro Pachón mirror $\rightarrow$ fiber cable $\rightarrow$ a student's glowing laptop cursor in 60 seconds flat.
* **Best For:** Youth inspiration shorts, STEM equity features, and museum interactives.

## PART 3: VISUAL METAPHORS, SOUND DESIGN & COLOR PALETTE

### Signature Visual Motifs
1. **The "378-Television Wall" Scale Reveal:**
   * *Framing:* Starts framed tightly on a spiral galaxy filling one 4K screen, then pulls back across a vast dark hangar of 378 tiled screens—all part of *one* 30-second Rubin exposure.
   * *Impact:* Makes 3,200 megapixels viscerally comprehensible to non-technical viewers in three seconds.
2. **The Blink-Comparator Step of *2026 ED138*:**
   * *Framing:* Classic astronomical magic reborn in 8K: background galaxies stay stone-still across three nights while one icy outer world steps steadily across the void.
   * *Impact:* Connects Clyde Tombaugh's 1930 Pluto discovery directly to 2026 algorithmic sky cinema.
3. **The 60-Second Photon Relay:**
   * *Framing:* Continuous kinetic camera following ancient starlight bouncing off Rubin's ternary mirror into SLAC's CCDs and popping onto a teenager's Zooniverse browser before a stopwatch hits `01:00`.
   * *Impact:* Visually proves zero-paywall scientific democracy in action.
4. **Dark-Sky Mirror Harmony:**
   * *Framing:* Low-reflectance dielectric satellite wing passing quietly without washing out a faint 26th-magnitude dwarf galaxy underneath.
