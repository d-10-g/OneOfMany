## PART 1: THREE NARRATIVE ANGLES & DIRECTOR HOOKS

### Angle A: "X-Raying the Engine of a Category 5 Typhoon" (Piercing the Eyewall)
* **Core Hook:** Shows an optical weather satellite helplessly staring at flat white cloud tops while a tropical storm secretly sucks up warm ocean moisture underneath—then switches to **COSMIC-2 / Spire / PlanetiQ** GNSS radio rays slicing horizontally *right through the raging eyewall* to hand AI weather models a **72-hour rapid-intensification warning**!
* **Emotional Arc:** Terror of overnight surprise hurricanes (like Otis) $\rightarrow$ Cleverness of bent L-band radio physics $\rightarrow$ Calm, daylight hospital evacuations saving coastal lives.
* **Visual Signature:** Golden atomic-clock radio beams curving like laser scalpels sideways through dark storm clouds, lighting up a 3D vertical moisture core in vivid teal.
* **Best For:** Weather/climate documentaries, coastal resilience briefings, and broad family audiences.

### Angle B: "Hearing a Tsunami 300 Kilometers Up in the Sky" (*NASA JPL GUARDIAN*)
* **Core Hook:** An undersea fault snaps in the pitch-black Pacific; on the ocean surface, the tsunami wave is barely 40 cm tall—yet it pushes an invisible air piston straight up at the speed of sound that **amplifies $10,000\times$ in thin air at $300\text{ km}$**, rippling GPS beams on **JPL's *GUARDIAN*** map in 8 minutes flat!
* **Emotional Arc:** Dread of silent mid-ocean tsunamis $\rightarrow$ Jaw-dropping atmospheric acoustics $\rightarrow$ Sirens and phones chiming an hour before waves reach shore.
* **Visual Signature:** Subtle blue ocean swell launching a giant translucent acoustic dome straight up into the glowing purple ionosphere where concentric ripple rings light up live!
* **Best For:** High-tension science shorts, Pacific/Indian Ocean community features, and geophysics fans.

### Angle C: "Erasing Airplane Contrails With a 2,000-Foot Nudge" (Bonus Climate Lever)
* **Core Hook:** Reveals that thin persistent contrails warm Earth nearly as much as jet fuel $\text{CO}_2$—and shows how $100\text{-meter}$-sharp GNSS-RO humidity slices let just 3% of airliners step $2,000\text{ feet}$ up or down out of wet ice layers, wiping warming contrails off the sky for pennies!
* **Emotional Arc:** Everyday guilt seeing crisscrossed contrail haze $\rightarrow$ Crisp vertical humidity map $\rightarrow$ Crystal-blue sky behind a cleanly cruising airliner.
* **Visual Signature:** Split sky: top jet trails a spreading white cirrus blanket in a wet layer; bottom jet nudges $2,000\text{ ft}$ into dry blue air with zero persistent trail!
* **Best For:** Aviation sustainability forums, climate-optimism reels, and travelers.

   * *Impact:* Makes radio occultation refractivity unmistakable in a single geometric glance.

   * *Framing:* Vertical cross-section from $4\text{ km}$ abyssal trench up to $300\text{ km}$ ionosphere showing a tiny sea swell blooming into giant, unmistakable electron rings overhead.
   * *Impact:* Explains why looking *up* at GPS plasma catches open-ocean tsunamis faster than sparse seafloor buoys.
