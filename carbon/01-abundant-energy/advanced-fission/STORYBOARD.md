# VISUAL STORYBOARD & INTERACTIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE
## Carbon Pillar 1: Walk-Away-Safe Gen IV Fission & Microreactors

> **Production Intent:**
> A reassuring, tactile visual narrative that replaces Cold War cooling-tower clichés with billiard-ball-sized
> melt-proof ceramic pebbles, atmospheric salt loops, and proud Wyoming coal-town families leading the clean-energy transition.

---

### Part I: Three Creative Openings

#### 1. The Joyful Human Story ("From Wyoming Coal Dust to Molten-Salt Sunset")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Golden dusk over Kemmerer, Wyoming: retired coal silos fade behind a crisp, low-profile steel hall at TerraPower's
  *Natrium* site. Inside, former coal operator Kendra Halvorsen smiles as insulated molten-salt tanks open to meet
  dinner-hour grid demand—then smash-cut to an Alaskan Inupiat village under northern stars where noisy diesel drums
  stand silent beside a warm, quiet container microreactor heating an indoor strawberry classroom.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Kendra's dad shoveled Wyoming coal for thirty-four winters. Today, fifty yards away, Kendra runs a zero-smoke
  salt battery reactor—and nobody in town had to pack up and leave."*

#### 2. The Cynic-Buster Reveal ("Turn Off All the Pumps—And Walk Away")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Inside an Idaho National Laboratory test cell, a gloved engineer holds a matte-gray **TRISO** fuel pebble the size
  of a billiard ball. On screen, operators intentionally shut off every cooling pump on a live reactor testbed:
  temperature climbs to $1,600^\circ\text{C}$, hits a natural physics wall, and drifts calmly back down while the
  ceramic pebble sits completely unscathed.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"What happens if every pump and backup wire fails inside a modern pebble-bed reactor? Nothing melts. Physics
  simply puts the core to sleep while operators walk out for coffee."*

#### 3. The Global Proof Documentary ("How France Decarbonized in 15 Years")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Fast archival-to-modern montage of 1970s–1980s France rolling out 56 standardized reactors like TGV high-speed
  trains—paired with a live European grid carbon map where France glows deep emerald green (`<50g CO₂/kWh`) next
  to coal-burning neighbors, then cutting to Oak Ridge, Tennessee where Kairos Power pours concrete for Google's fleet.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"People say clean baseload takes half a century. France decarbonized an entire industrial nation in fifteen years
  by building one standardized design on repeat—and now Gen IV fits on a semi-truck."*

### Part III: Interactive Infographic & Visual Metaphor Bank

1. **The $1,800^\circ\text{C}$ TRISO Blowtorch & Walk-Away Simulator:**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Hold a virtual TRISO pebble under simulated core loss-of-coolant conditions. Watch peak core temperature
     physically plateau around `1,600°C` due to Doppler reactivity feedback while the silicon-carbide shell rating
     sits untouched above `1,800°C`—zero fission-product release, zero pumps, zero operator buttons clicked.
   * **Color & Sound Cue:**
     Warm amber glow softly dims back to calm silver-gray accompanied by a tranquil, steady heartbeat pulse.
2. **Pressure Gauge Showdown (`2,200 psi` Espresso Boiler vs. `15 psi` Soup Pot):**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Compare steel vessel thickness, concrete containment volume, and factory shippability between a 1975
     pressurized water reactor (`150 atm`) and a 2026 molten-salt or sodium core (`1 atm`). Toggle *"Puncture Pipe"*
     to see pressurized steam flash vs. molten salt calmly freezing solid inside a catch pan.
   * **Visual Payoff:**
     Shows visually why 70% of legacy nuclear concrete and steel cost simply disappears at atmospheric pressure.
3. **The Cola-Can Lifetime Waste Visualizer (+ Fast-Reactor Recycler):**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Display one person's entire 80-year electricity footprint as a single `12 oz soda can` of solid ceramic nuclear
     fuel—compared beside a `700-ton mountain` of coal ash and $\text{CO}_2$. Then click *"Run Through Oklo Fast Reactor"*
     to extract 20x more clean power and shrink decay horizon to `~300 years`.
   * **Data Layer:**
     Calculates how existing U.S. spent-fuel casks alone could power all of America for >100 years without mining an ounce of uranium.
4. **Arctic & Island Diesel-to-Microreactor Payback Map:**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Click off-grid diesel communities (*Kotzebue AK, Iqaluit Nunavut, Pacific Atolls, Remote Mining Hubs*) to compare
     `$0.65–$1.10/kWh` diesel barge power and black-carbon soot against a drop-in container microreactor.
   * **Takeaway Card:**
     `Delivered by Truck. Cooled by Air. Safe by Physics.`
