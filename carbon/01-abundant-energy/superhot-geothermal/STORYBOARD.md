# VISUAL STORYBOARD & INTERACTIVE PRODUCTION GUIDE
## Carbon Pillar 1: Everywhere Baseload: Enhanced & Superhot Rock Geothermal

> **Production Intent:**
> A grounded, gritty-meets-gorgeous visual journey showing how Texas and Utah roughnecks turned oilfield diamond
> drills toward Earth's inexhaustible $400^\circ\text{C}+$ thermal heart—heating snowy towns, growing winter
> berries, and powering 24/7 clean grids without a single smokestack.

### Part I: Three Creative Openings

#### 1. The Joyful Human Story ("Shrimp & Tomatoes in a Utah Blizzard")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Heavy January snow falls silently over Milford, Utah, yet Main Street's sidewalks steam dry and ice-free.
  Directional driller Wyatt Callahan walks past laughing kids into a warm, glowing glasshouse bursting with
  red heirloom tomatoes and tropical shrimp tanks—then camera tilts down three kilometers through solid granite
  to reveal glowing turquoise water sweeping heat out of horizontal rock fractures at Fervo's Cape Station.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Wyatt spent fifteen years drilling Texas oil wells. Today, his diamond drill bits tap pure Utah granite
  heat—and his hometown grows fresh tomatoes in a January blizzard."*

#### 2. The Cynic-Buster Reveal ("The 99% Hot Planet Beneath Your Shoes")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Camera pulls back from a suburban backyard lawn until Earth slices open like a glowing peach: a paper-thin
  cool crust skin floating atop a massive $1,000^\circ\text{C}$ incandescent mantle. An animated caliper pinches
  the crust (*"Just 6 miles down!"*) while Fervo's 2026 Cape Station grid-sync meter and \$2.2B IPO ticker flash live.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Ninety-nine percent of our planet is hotter than a pizza oven. We didn't lack clean baseload energy—we just
  needed oilfield horizontal drills to reach six miles beneath our shoes."*

#### 3. The Global Proof Documentary ("Reykjavik Under Every City")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Archival black-and-white 1932 footage of Reykjavik blanketed in black coal soot dissolves seamlessly into vibrant
  modern Reykjavik outdoor geothermal pools, then cuts to towering electric rigs in Geretsried, Bavaria (Eavor-Loop)
  and Beaver County, Utah.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Ninety years ago, Iceland traded coal smoke for hot rock and became one of the richest, cleanest societies on
  Earth. Now closed-loop and superhot drilling bring Iceland's secret under Munich, Houston, and Tokyo."*

### Part III: Interactive Infographic & Visual Metaphor Bank

1. **The "Drill Beneath Your Zip Code" Depth Explorer:**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Type any city on Earth (*Chicago, Munich, Nairobi, Tokyo, Dallas*). A vertical 3D borehole camera plunges
     beneath local soil and bedrock, lighting up exact crustal temperatures at `3 km` (district heating),
     `5 km` (enhanced geothermal binary power), and `12 km` ($400^\circ\text{C}+$ supercritical superhot rock).
   * **Visual Payoff:**
     Displays how many tiny two-acre well pads would power 100% of that city's electricity and winter heating.
2. **Normal Steam vs. Supercritical $400^\circ\text{C}$ Multiplier Gauge:**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Drag rock temperature from $180^\circ\text{C}$ across the critical point ($374^\circ\text{C}$) up to $450^\circ\text{C}$.
     Watch fluid density and low viscosity merge into a golden supercritical state as the single-well turbine meter
     jumps from `5 MWe` up to `40+ MWe`—explaining why superhot rock slashes LCOE toward \$20/MWh.
   * **Color & Sound Cue:**
     Subtle rushing steam shifts into a smooth, turbine-like harmonic hum as supercritical enthalpy unlocks.
3. **Surface Footprint Showdown (500 MW Firm Power):**
   * **User Mechanic:**
     Overlay a 500 MW firm-power land polygon onto a familiar city park map: toggle between *Coal Strip Mine*,
     *Biomass Plantation*, *Natural Gas Wellfield*, and *Cape-Class Horizontal Geothermal Pad* (a tiny green square
     leaving 98% of surrounding sagebrush untouched).
   * **Data Layer:**
     Compares lifecycle land disturbance, water consumption (zero net in binary loops), and 40-year local tax revenue.
4. **Oil-Rig-to-Geothermal Skill Match Wheel:**
