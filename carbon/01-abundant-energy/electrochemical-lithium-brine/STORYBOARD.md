* **Visual & Audio:**
  Dramatic split-screen zoom: on the left, a sprawling 10,000-acre neon-green evaporation lagoon baking away ancient water
  for 500 days while losing half its lithium to salt sludge; on the right, brine rushes through a clear glass tube packed
  with tiny white Lilac ceramic beads—catching 92% of lithium in **90 minutes** on **1/50th the land**.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Critics still share photos of giant desert brine ponds from twenty years ago. Today, molecular ceramic sieves do the
  job two hundred times faster inside a building—and give the water right back."*

#### 3. The Global Proof Documentary ("375 Million EVs Beneath One California Lake")
* **Visual & Audio:**
  Drone glides over steaming geothermal plants beside California's Salton Sea, cutting to former oilfield drillers in El
  Dorado, Arkansas turning a brine valve at Standard Lithium's Smackover plant and pink Andean flamingos wading undisturbed
  in protected high-altitude lagoons.
* **Opening Line:**
  *"Just one geothermal basin in Southern California holds enough dissolved lithium for every passenger car in America—and
  closed-loop extraction leaves surface water untouched."*
